SEO Desk support

SEO checks for the page you're on and the pages you write: tags, speed, links, schema, robots.txt, snippets and UTMs.

The 9 tools

  • Page check: Title, description, headings, social tags, structured data and links, with plain-English fixes. Free: every finding, title and description pixel widths, the heading outline, social tags with the share card, link counts. Pro: structured data checks, hreflang, the full link list with CSV, a search result preview, Markdown and CSV reports, snapshots to compare after changes.
  • Speed: Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP), load timing and page weight, with the elements that caused them. Free: all five metrics with their causes, load timing, page weight, the 5 largest files and every fix. Pro: reload and measure (cold cache), outlines on the page, every file with CSV, history with before and after, an HTML report.
  • Broken links: Broken links, redirects and missing #anchors on your own pages. Free: up to 100 links per page: OK, redirected, broken or timed out, outlined on the page. Pro: every link plus images, scripts and stylesheets, #anchor checks, a 50-page crawl of your site, CSV and HTML export, ignore patterns.
  • Compare pages: Staging against live, or a page before and after a deploy: what changed in text, SEO tags, links, images and headings. Free: text and SEO tag changes with warnings (noindex appeared, a canonical pointing at a test server, lost description). Pro: link, image and heading changes, ignore rules, saved snapshots, Markdown, HTML and CSV export.
  • Tech stack: Which CMS, frameworks, analytics and payment tools a site uses, read from the page itself. Free: every technology found, by category, with versions and the signals that matched. Pro: history of sites you checked with notes, compare two sites, CSV, JSON and Markdown export, your own detection rules.
  • Titles & descriptions: Write titles and meta descriptions for many pages, with pixel widths, a result preview and duplicate checks. Free: one project of 10 pages with every count, check and the preview. Pro: unlimited pages and projects, CSV import and export, brand suffix templates, find and replace, client reports.
  • Schema markup: Write JSON-LD structured data from forms, checked against Google’s documented requirements. Free: FAQ, Organization, Breadcrumbs and Article markup, checked and ready to copy. Pro: all 14 types, several items linked in one @graph, templates per site, bulk JSON-LD from CSV, import existing markup.
  • Robots.txt: Test robots.txt rules: allowed or blocked for each address and crawler, with the deciding line explained. Free: 20 addresses and 3 crawlers at a time, explanations and the full lint. Pro: fetch the live robots.txt, unlimited addresses, every crawler as a matrix, the AI crawler view, compare two versions, saved tests, CSV.
  • UTM links: Tag the page you’re on with UTM parameters, keep naming consistent and get a QR code. Free: build and copy links, custom parameters, 3 presets, naming rules and a QR code. Pro: unlimited presets, required fields and allowed values, QR download as PNG or SVG, link history with CSV, shared presets.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to SEO Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.
