Big Tally support

A tally counter with big buttons and big numbers. Several named counters, any step size, undo, a timed history of every change, and CSV export through the share menu. The screen stays on; volume buttons can count. No ads, no internet, everything stays on your device.

Get it

  • Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets: search for "Big Tally" in the Galaxy Store.
  • Fire tablets: search for "Big Tally" in the Amazon Appstore.

Bigger text and colors

Open Settings in the app to choose the text size, light or dark colors, and high contrast. The app also follows your device's own font size (Settings → Display → Font size).

New device

Sign in to the same store account on the new device and download Big Tally again from your library at no charge.

Refunds

  • Purchases are handled by Samsung. To ask for a refund, open Galaxy Store → Menu → Orders, or contact Samsung through the Galaxy Store help pages.
  • Purchases are handled by Amazon. To ask for a refund, contact Amazon customer service (amazon.com → Your Account → Your Apps & Devices, or Help → Contact us).
