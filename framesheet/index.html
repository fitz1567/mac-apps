Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Framesheet does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing about you or your work is sent to us.

This policy explains how Framesheet (“the app”), a Mac app that lays out a folder of photos as a captioned PDF photo report, with a cover, a photo index, sections and before/after pairs, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Framesheet does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac only:

  • Your report: the copies of the photos you add, the date, time, GPS position and camera read from each photo, your captions and notes, the sections and pairs, the street addresses looked up (only if you turned that on), and the report’s details and settings, in Framesheet’s own folder on your Mac
  • Your company name, contact lines and logo, and the templates you save
  • A few display preferences (the preview size, the settings tab)
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

The report is in Framesheet’s own folder on your Mac (inside its App Sandbox container), preferences are in Framesheet’s preferences and the license key in your Keychain. Framesheet keeps its own copy of each photo you add and never changes the originals. The PDFs and ZIPs you export are new files, written only where you choose. Start a New Report to delete Framesheet’s copies.

Your photos

Photos you add from your library come through Apple’s Photos picker: Framesheet gets copies of only the photos you choose, read-only, and never sees the rest of your library. It doesn’t ask for Photos access, and it doesn’t take photos.

Your files

Framesheet runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, so it can open only the photos and folders you pick or drop on it, and save only where you choose.

On your Mac

Reading each photo’s date, time and GPS position, the captions, the layout and the PDF all happen on your Mac. No photo is uploaded anywhere.

Street addresses

Off unless you turn on Look up street addresses. When it’s on, the GPS position of each photo (just the coordinates, never the photo) is sent to Apple’s map service to find the street address, as Apple’s Maps does; photos taken at the same spot share one lookup. Apple’s privacy policy covers that request. With it off, places show as coordinates and nothing is sent.

Network

Framesheet is sold directly through Gumroad. Besides the optional address lookup, its only network requests are the license check with Gumroad (your license key, when you enter it and about once a week) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com. Neither sends anything about your photos or your clients.

Updates and license checks

Framesheet, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, which sees only the app’s version and, like any website, your internet address, and it downloads an update only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates). When you enter a license key, Framesheet sends that key to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again about once a week; that is all it sends to Gumroad. Neither sends anything about you, your files or how you use the app.

Purchases

Framesheet is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. The app sends your license key to Gumroad to check it (when you enter it and about once a week); that is all it sends to Gumroad.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
