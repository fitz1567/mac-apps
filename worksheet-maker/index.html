Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Worksheet Maker does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Worksheet Maker (“the app”), a Mac app that makes printable practice worksheets for Kindergarten through 8th grade, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Worksheet Maker does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac only:

  • Your last choices (grade, subject, how many questions, answer key and mixing), so they come back next time

It stays in Worksheet Maker’s own storage on your Mac and is never sent to us. Worksheets are saved only where you choose, or sent to your printer through macOS.

Offline

Worksheet Maker makes every worksheet on your Mac, offline. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check with Gumroad (your license key, when you enter it and about once a week) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com. Neither sends anything about your worksheets.

Purchases

Worksheet Maker is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. The app sends your license key to Gumroad to check it, and nothing else.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
