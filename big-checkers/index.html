Big Checkers privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Big Checkers is an Android app by Kevin Fitzgerald for Fire tablets and Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets. It does not collect your data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no server of ours. The app has no permission to use the internet at all.

What it keeps on your device

  • The game in progress and your record against the computer (wins, losses, draws for each level)
  • Your settings (game choices, board options, text size, colors)

This stays in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you turn on your device's own backup, Android may include it in that backup, which is covered by your device maker's privacy policy.

Permissions

None. The app asks for no Android permissions.

Links that leave the app

The About screen can open this page in your browser, the app's page in the Amazon Appstore and Galaxy Store, or your email app to write to support. Those apps handle anything you do there.

Purchases

Big Checkers is a one-time purchase in the Amazon Appstore and Galaxy Store, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Amazon's privacy notice covers that purchase. Samsung's privacy notice covers that purchase.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is collected, so nothing is sold or shared.

Children

Big Checkers isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date.
