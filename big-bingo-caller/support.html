Calling

How do I call a number?

Tap Call Next (or press the space bar on a Mac or iPad keyboard). The number is drawn at random from those not yet called, shown big, spoken out loud and lit on the board. Tap Undo to take back the last call.

I can’t hear the calls.

Check that Say each call out loud is on in Settings and turn the volume up. On iPhone and iPad, calls play even with the ring switch on silent. For a big room, connect a speaker, or turn on Say it twice.

Can everyone see the board?

Yes: mirror your iPhone or iPad to a TV with AirPlay (Screen Mirroring in Control Center). On a Mac, make the window full screen.

How do the patterns work?

Tap the pattern under the ball to choose one: straight line, four corners, letter X, postage stamp, picture frame or blackout. It shows everyone what to play for, and Check a Card uses it.

Unlock Everything

What does the purchase add?

Two extras: Check a Card, which shows any card from the Large Print Bingo Cards book with the called numbers circled and says whether it has the pattern, and Auto Call, which calls a new number every 5 to 30 seconds until you pause it. Calling is free.

Which cards can I check?

The 100 cards in the Large Print Bingo Cards book and its printable edition, by the card number printed on each one. For other cards, read the winner’s numbers back against the board.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
