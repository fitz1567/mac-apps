Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Resale Ready does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Resale Ready (“the app”), a Mac app that tests a Mac you're selling and makes a condition report, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Resale Ready does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • The current test run: the results, your answers to the checks by hand, and the notes you write for the report
  • If you have Pro, the reports you save to History, including ones you add from your other Macs
  • Your license key once you enter one, and its license status (such as a free trial’s start date), in your Mac’s Keychain

It stays in Resale Ready’s own storage on your Mac and is never sent to us. A PDF, printout, text or report file goes only where you save, print or paste it.

What it reads about your Mac

To fill in the report, Resale Ready reads this Mac’s model, serial number, chip, memory, macOS version and the size of its startup drive. On a MacBook it also reads the battery’s charge cycle count, capacity and condition. It runs macOS’s own profiles tool to see whether the Mac is enrolled in device management, and it lists connected USB devices while the USB ports test is open. None of this leaves your Mac unless you put it in a report you share.

Camera and microphone

Resale Ready asks for the camera or microphone only when you use its test, and uses them only while that test is open: the camera shows a live picture, and the microphone drives a level meter. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. You can turn either off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The keyboard and trackpad tests

While the keyboard test runs, Resale Ready notes which keys you press in its own window, so they can turn green; it doesn’t keep what you type and never sees typing in other apps. The trackpad test notes where your fingers touch inside its test area.

Network

What it reads about your Mac and your test results never leave your Mac unless you share a report. Resale Ready is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com.

Updates and license checks

Resale Ready, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Resale Ready contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Resale Ready is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
