Resale Ready

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Resale Ready does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Resale Ready (“the app”), a Mac app that tests a Mac you're selling and makes a condition report, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Resale Ready does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac only:

  • The current test run: the results, your answers to the checks by hand, and the notes you write for the report
  • If you have Pro, the reports you save to History, including ones you add from your other Macs

It stays in Resale Ready’s own storage on your Mac and is never sent to us. A PDF, printout, text or report file goes only where you save, print or paste it.

What it reads about your Mac

To fill in the report, Resale Ready reads this Mac’s model, serial number, chip, memory, macOS version and the size of its startup drive. It runs macOS’s own profiles tool to see whether the Mac is enrolled in device management, and it lists connected USB devices while the USB ports test is open. None of this leaves your Mac unless you put it in a report you share.

Camera and microphone

Resale Ready asks for the camera or microphone only when you use its test, and uses them only while that test is open: the camera shows a live picture, and the microphone drives a level meter. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. You can turn either off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The keyboard and trackpad tests

While the keyboard test runs, Resale Ready notes which keys you press in its own window, so they can turn green; it doesn’t keep what you type and never sees typing in other apps. The trackpad test notes where your fingers touch inside its test area.

Network

What it reads about your Mac and your test results never leave your Mac unless you share a report. Resale Ready is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check with Gumroad (your license key, when you enter it and about once a week) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com.

Purchases

Resale Ready is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. The app sends your license key to Gumroad to check it, and nothing else.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
