Deals and fares

Where do the fares come from?

From Aviasales (through the Travelpayouts partner program): the cheapest fares travelers found in recent searches, collected each night for the busiest U.S. airports. They’re a guide; the booking site shows the live price.

Why are today’s fares not there yet?

The fares are collected overnight, but some mornings the update doesn’t arrive until late morning Eastern time (the nightly job can start late). Until then FunBound shows the previous night’s fares with the day each was seen. Pull down on Deals to check again.

Why is my airport missing from Deals?

FunBound follows fares from the busiest U.S. airports. Pick a nearby one in Settings (up to three), or use the Disney World and Universal Orlando planners, which estimate typical fares for any airport.

What does the dot on a calendar day mean?

That fare was last seen more than three days ago, so it may have changed. Fares seen more than a week ago aren’t shown.

Is FunBound free?

Yes. When you book a flight through FunBound’s links, the travel site pays FunBound a small commission. You pay the same price.

Price alerts and the widget

How do price alerts work?

Open a place and tap Watch this price (or Watch prices around these dates on a Disney World trip), then choose a target price or any drop. FunBound compares the watch with each night’s fares on your device and sends a notification when the price falls. The bell at the top of every tab lists your watches.

I don’t get price alert notifications.

Check Settings › Notifications › FunBound, and turn on Background App Refresh (Settings › General) so FunBound can check while it’s closed. It always checks when you open it.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for FunBound. It shows the best deal from your airports or the first trip you’re watching. If FunBound hasn’t refreshed for a few days, the widget says when its prices are from; after a week it shows none until you open the app.

Some fares say Low, Typical or High. What does that mean?

Once FunBound has at least 21 nights of price history for a route, it compares a fare with the cheapest prices found each night over the last 90 days. Past prices don’t predict future ones.

Safe & Fun

Where do the safety levels come from?

From the U.S. State Department’s travel advisories, downloaded daily: Level 1 (exercise normal precautions) to Level 4 (do not travel). Always read the full advisory before you book; they can change quickly. If FunBound can’t reach the State Department and has no saved copy, a place says “Advisory unavailable”.

The Disney World and Universal Orlando planners

How do they find the cheapest time to go?

Each prices the whole trip (flights, a hotel, park tickets, add-ons and the ride from the airport) for every check-in date it has prices for, within the limits you set, and shows the cheapest months and days.

Are the prices exact?

No. Disney doesn’t offer a price feed, so FunBound uses Disney’s published room rates, ticket prices and add-on prices as estimates. Universal publishes “from” prices (the lowest; prices vary by day), so its hotels and tickets cost the same on every date in FunBound and are marked “From price”. Disney’s and Universal’s websites show the final price for your dates.

What happened to FastPass?

Disney replaced it with Lightning Lane: Multi Pass (three rides booked ahead each day), Single Pass (for the most popular rides) and Premier Pass. The Disney World planner can include any of them.
