Deals and fares

Where do the fares come from?

From Aviasales (through the Travelpayouts partner program): the cheapest fares travelers found in recent searches, collected each night for the busiest U.S. airports. They’re a guide; the booking site shows the live price.

Why is my airport missing from Deals?

FunBound follows fares from the busiest U.S. airports. Pick a nearby one in Settings, or use the Disney World planner, which estimates typical fares for any airport.

Is FunBound free?

Yes. When you book a flight through FunBound’s links, the travel site pays FunBound a small commission. You pay the same price.

Safe & Fun

Where do the safety levels come from?

From the U.S. State Department’s travel advisories, downloaded daily: Level 1 (exercise normal precautions) to Level 4 (do not travel). Always read the full advisory before you book; they can change quickly.

The Disney World planner

How does it find the cheapest time to go?

It prices the whole trip (flights, a Disney resort, park tickets, Park Hopper, Lightning Lane and the ride from the airport) for every check-in date Disney has priced, within the limits you set, and shows the cheapest month and days.

Are the prices exact?

No. Disney doesn’t offer a price feed, so FunBound uses Disney’s published room rates, ticket prices and add-on prices as estimates. Disney’s website shows the final price for your dates.

What happened to FastPass?

Disney replaced it with Lightning Lane: Multi Pass (three rides booked ahead each day), Single Pass (for the most popular rides) and Premier Pass. The planner can include any of them.
