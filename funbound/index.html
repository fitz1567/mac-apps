Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: FunBound does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how FunBound (“the app”), a travel deals and Orlando trip planning app for the Mac (and, earlier, iPhone and iPad), developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. FunBound does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac (or your iPhone or iPad)

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac (or your iPhone or iPad) only:

  • Your home airports, your safety setting and your Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando trip searches, so they’re there next time
  • Your price watches (the places or Disney World dates you watch, your target prices and the last price seen), your packing lists, and the trip dates you enter for passport reminders
  • The fares, price calendars, price histories, price guide updates and travel advisories it last downloaded, so it works without a connection
  • For What to Wear: your temperature unit, activities and laundry setting, the packing items you tick off, and the typical weather it downloaded for the places you looked up
  • On iPhone and iPad only: a small summary for the FunBound widget (the best deal from your airports and the first trip you’re watching), kept in a container on your device that only FunBound and its widget can read

It stays in FunBound’s own storage (on the Mac, its sandbox container in ~/Library/Containers) and is never sent to us. It is removed if you delete the app and its data.

What FunBound downloads

Each night’s fares, price calendars and 90-night price histories for your home airports, and updates to the Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando price guides and Disney’s event dates, from FunBound’s data website (the same files for everyone who picks those airports, so they don’t reveal who you are); and travel advisories from the U.S. State Department’s public website. These are plain file downloads: nothing about you, your watches or your plans is sent with them. The map is drawn by Apple Maps; FunBound doesn’t use your location.

What to Wear weather

When you look up a place in What to Wear, FunBound finds the city in a list built into the app and uses typical weather built into the app for about 225 popular places. For any other city it asks NASA’s POWER service (power.larc.nasa.gov) once for that city’s typical weather by its map coordinates, and when your trip starts within about nine days it asks the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no) for the forecast. Those requests carry only the city’s coordinates, never your name, an account or your own location; like any website, those services see your internet address. Typical weather: NASA POWER Project. Forecasts: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0). City list: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

Notifications

Price alerts and passport reminders are local notifications. FunBound compares your watches with the downloaded fares on your device and schedules the notification there; nothing is sent to a server to do it. On the Mac it checks when you open it, when its window comes forward and every few hours while it’s open; on iPhone and iPad also in the background if Background App Refresh is on. You’re asked for permission the first time you set a watch or a passport reminder, and you can turn notifications off in System Settings › Notifications › FunBound (on iPhone and iPad, Settings › Notifications › FunBound).

Links to other websites

When you click a booking or information link, the website opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies: Aviasales for flights; Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando and Mears Connect for parks, hotels and airport rides; the U.S. State Department and TSA for advisories, passports and ID; and the price sources listed in FunBound’s Settings. Flight links include FunBound’s partner code, which tells the site the booking came from FunBound so it can pay FunBound a commission; it contains nothing about you.

Partner links in the future

FunBound may later add hotel and rental car links through the Travelpayouts partner program (for example Trip.com or DiscoverCars). They will work the same way: a partner code in the link, nothing about you, and the partner’s own privacy policy once you’re on its site. This page will name them before they appear in the app.

Updates, download and tips

The Mac app checks fitztoppicks.com for updates; that request carries only the app’s version and your macOS version. FunBound for Mac is a free download through Gumroad, which handles any payment you choose to make; tips through Ko-fi are optional and handled by Ko-fi. FunBound has no license key and never contacts Gumroad or Ko-fi itself.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
