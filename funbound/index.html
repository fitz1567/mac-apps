The short version: FunBound does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how FunBound (“the app”), a travel deals app for iPhone and iPad, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. FunBound does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your home airport, your safety setting and your Disney World trip search, so they’re there next time
  • The last fares and travel advisories it downloaded, so it works without a connection

It stays in FunBound’s own storage on your device and is never sent to us. It is removed if you delete the app.

What FunBound downloads

Fares for your home airport from our website (the same file for everyone who picks that airport, so it doesn’t reveal who you are) and travel advisories from the U.S. State Department’s public website. Nothing about you is sent with these requests.

Booking links

When you tap a booking link, the travel site (Aviasales, Disney or Mears Connect) opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies. Flight links include FunBound’s partner code, which tells the site the booking came from FunBound so it can pay FunBound a commission; it contains nothing about you.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
