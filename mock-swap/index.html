Mock Swap privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Mock Swap is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your mocks: the request addresses, the responses you typed and which ones are on
  • Your scenarios
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Mock Swap makes no network requests of its own except checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks. Mocks are applied by Chrome's request rules on this computer.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Mock Swap sends the key and Mock Swap's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: Lets Chrome answer the requests the user picked with the mock response they saved (a redirect to a data: address) or fail them to simulate an outage. Chrome applies the rules itself; the extension never reads requests, responses or pages, and the rules only take effect on sites the user has granted.
  • storage: Keeps the user's mocks and scenarios on this computer.
  • http://*/* and https://*/* (optional): Requested one site at a time, only when the user saves a mock for that site (for example http://localhost or api.example.com). Chrome only applies a mock on sites the extension may access. Nothing is requested at install.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Mock Swap isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
