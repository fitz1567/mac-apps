Playing

How do I play?

Tap the dots that match the MATCH color before time runs out. Each level adds dots, moves them and brings in look-alike shades. Classic gives you 3 lives.

What are the modes?

Classic and the Daily Challenge (the same boards for everyone each day, with streaks) are free. Time Attack (one 60-second clock), Zen (no timer, no lives) and Hardcore (one life, less time) come with ChromaPop+, and each has one free try a day.

I’m color-blind. Can I play?

Yes, and it’s free. Turn on Colorblind Mode on Home or Pause: every color gets its own symbol. Under it, Colors switches to dot sets picked to stay apart for red-green color blindness (Deutan and Protan). If Differentiate Without Color is on in iOS Settings, symbols turn on by themselves.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. The game follows your device’s language (English or Spanish).

Scores and Game Center

Why don’t my scores show on the leaderboard?

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings › Game Center, then finish a game. Only your first Daily Challenge run each day is ranked.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Progress is kept on each device. Game Center leaderboards and achievements follow your Game Center account.

ChromaPop+

What does ChromaPop+ add?

Unlimited Time Attack, Zen and Hardcore. It is one purchase with no subscription. If you bought ChromaPop back when it was a paid app, ChromaPop+ is already yours.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
