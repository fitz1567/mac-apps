Teacher Gift Tags

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Teacher Gift Tags does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Teacher Gift Tags (“the app”), an app for designing and printing gift tags for teachers on iPhone, iPad and Mac, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Teacher Gift Tags does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • The tags and class cards you make: their words, the To and From names, colors, stickers and settings
  • Pictures you add to a tag (a photo or your child’s drawing), made smaller and saved without location data
  • Teachers you add: name, role, school year, an optional birthday (month and day only), reminder settings and notes
  • Class lists you type or paste: the children’s names
  • Your settings: paper size, units, reminder time and your printer correction
  • The upcoming birthdays (names and month and day) for the Teacher Gift Tags widget, kept in a container on your device that only the app and its widget can read

It stays in Teacher Gift Tags’ own storage on your device and is never sent to us. If iCloud is on for Teacher Gift Tags, it is also kept in your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices; that copy belongs to your Apple Account and we can’t see it. It is removed if you delete the app (and from iCloud if you remove the app’s data in iCloud settings).

Children’s names

Class lists and the names on tags and class cards are typed or pasted in by you. They are used only to put names on the tags and cards you print or share. They stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and are never sent to us or anyone else. Teacher Gift Tags is made for parents, room parents and teachers, not for children to use.

Photos, camera and printing

Pictures come from the system photo picker, which shares only the pictures you choose, or from the camera when you tap Take a Picture of a Drawing. Saving an image to your photo library asks for add-only access. Printing uses AirPrint (or the Mac’s print panel) and goes straight to your printer. Files you share go only where you send them.

Reminders, widget and Shortcuts

Birthday reminders are notifications created on your device, and the app asks permission only when you turn a reminder on. The widget and the Shortcuts actions run on your device. None of them send data anywhere. The only network use is Apple’s: iCloud sync, if you have it on, and the App Store for the Pro purchase.

Purchases

Teacher Gift Tags is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
