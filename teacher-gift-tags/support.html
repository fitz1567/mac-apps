Printing

How do I make sure tags print at the right size?

In the print options, keep the scale at 100% (Actual Size), not “Fit to page”. To check your printer, open Settings › Printer Check and print the test page: the square should measure exactly 4 inches (10.16 cm). If it’s a little off, enter what you measured and the app makes up the difference.

Which label sheets does it fit?

Avery 22806 (2 in square), 22807 (2 in round), 22808 (2½ in round), 22802 (2 × 3½ in tags with strings), 5371 (2 × 3½ in business cards) and 5160 (1 × 2⅝ in labels), sized from Avery’s published templates. Choose Label sheet in Print & Share. If part of a sheet is used, set how many spots to skip. Avery is a trademark of Avery Products Corporation; Teacher Gift Tags isn’t affiliated with Avery.

My labels print a little off the die cuts.

Some printers feed paper slightly off. In Settings › Printer Check, move everything right or down in half-millimeter steps until the art lines up.

What paper should I use?

Card stock (65–110 lb) makes sturdy tags on most home printers. Choose Plain paper for tags you cut out: crop marks in the margins line up with every tag edge, and outlines are best for scissors and round tags.

Designing

How do I add a sticker?

In the editor, tap a sticker in Stickers to add it, then drag it on the tag. Tap it again to change its size or turn it, or to remove it.

Can I use my child’s drawing?

Yes, with Pro. Tap Add a Photo or Drawing or Take a Picture of a Drawing, then choose the Child’s drawing style to show the whole picture on a white mat.

Can I make my own size?

Yes, with Pro: Shape and size › Custom Size, in inches or millimeters, up to 8 × 10½ in.

Whole class and birthdays

How do I print a tag for every child?

With Pro, add the names in Class Lists (paste them, one per line or separated by commas), then tap Tags for the Whole Class. Each tag gets its own name in From or To.

How does the class card work?

With Pro, open a teacher in Teachers and tap Class Birthday Card, or make one from a class list. Add each child’s name and an optional short message; the names fit themselves to the card. Ask Families for Names writes a note you can send the class.

How do birthday reminders work?

Add a teacher’s birthday (month and day) in Teachers and turn on Remind me. You’ll get a notification on the day and, if you choose, a few days before. Change the time in Settings.
