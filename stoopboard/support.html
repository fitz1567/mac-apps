Need a hand?

Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn’t, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

Bin night

How do I add a pickup?

Tap + › Pickup, give it a name (Trash, Recycling, Yard waste), pick the day and how it repeats: every week, every other week (from any pickup date you know), certain weeks of the month, or only in some months.

How do holiday weeks work?

Many haulers move every pickup one day later in a week with a weekday holiday. Turn that on in Household › Holidays and moved pickups and choose which holidays your hauler observes. You can also move or cancel a single pickup there, and add your family’s own days off.

I didn’t get the reminder. What should I check?

Make sure the reminder is on in Household and that notifications are allowed for Stoopboard in Settings › Notifications. The reminder comes the evening before a pickup, at the time you chose.

Chores and the board

How do I add kids and chores?

Tap + › Kid, then + › Chore and pick the days. Check chores off in the Chores tab or right from the Chores widget.

How does the allowance work?

Set an amount per star in Household › Allowance. Stoopboard shows what each kid has earned and keeps a list of the payouts you record. It never moves money.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and pick Stoopboard. There are Lock Screen and StandBy widgets too. Household › See the widgets shows each one.

How do I share the board with my family?

Household › Invite family sends an iCloud invitation by Messages or Mail. Everyone who accepts sees the same bins, chores and notes, and can check things off. Each person keeps their own reminder time and calendar.

Stoopboard Pro

What’s free and what does Pro add?

Free: 2 pickup schedules, 2 kids, 3 notes and 3 countdowns, with the Bin Night, Chores and Fridge Board widgets in the Classic style. Pro is one purchase with no subscription: no limits, the Whole Board widget, and the Chalkboard and Corkboard styles. Family Sharing is on.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
