Repro Shot privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Repro Shot is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your bug reports, screenshots and annotations, in the extension's local database on this computer (the current report, or the last 100 with Pro), including the address of the page you captured; you can delete them in Settings
  • Your settings and report templates
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Repro Shot never uploads screenshots or reports and has no integration with any tracker. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Repro Shot sends the key and a device label (such as "Repro Shot on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Takes a screenshot of the current tab (chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab) and reads its address and size, only when the user clicks the toolbar button or presses its shortcut. Repro Shot never reads the page's content, console, network requests or cookies.
  • storage: Keeps settings, report templates and the license key on this computer. Reports and their screenshots are kept in the extension's own local database (the current one, or the last 100 with Pro).
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Repro Shot isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
