The short version: Remountwise does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Remountwise (“the app”), a Mac menu bar app that keeps your network shares mounted after sleep and Wi-Fi changes, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Remountwise does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your shares (addresses, user names, names and rules) and an activity log, in files in Remountwise’s own folder on your Mac
  • Share passwords and your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain
  • Your settings: the check interval, notifications and whether you’ve seen the welcome screen

Shares and the log are in ~/Library/Application Support/Remountwise, settings in its preferences, and passwords and the license key in your Keychain.

Your shares

Remountwise runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox because clearing a frozen network connection needs a forced unmount, which the sandbox doesn’t allow. It mounts shares through macOS’s own NetFS, looks at their folders in /Volumes with a short timeout, and knocks on the server’s file-sharing port to see whether it’s awake. Nothing about your shares or files is uploaded.

Wi-Fi name and location

Wi-Fi rules need the name of the network you’re on, which macOS gives only to apps allowed to use Location Services. Remountwise asks only when you add a Wi-Fi rule, reads the network name, and never reads or stores your location.

Local network

To see whether your file server is awake before it reconnects your shares, Remountwise knocks on the server’s file-sharing port on your local network, and looks up .local server names with Bonjour. On macOS 15 and later, macOS asks for your permission the first time; if you say no, Remountwise can’t tell whether the server is awake, and it tells you the setting is off. You can change your answer in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Nothing is sent anywhere but your own server.

Network

Remountwise is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only internet requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID), checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com and the list of our other Mac apps, downloaded from fitztoppicks.com when you open it (at most once a day; the same file for everyone). None of them sends anything about your shares.

Updates and license checks

Remountwise, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Remountwise contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Remountwise is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
