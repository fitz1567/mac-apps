Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn’t, email k.fitzgerald310@gmail.com with your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

Playing

What’s in it?

22 games in Math, Letters & Reading and Our World, with every question read aloud. Children answer by tapping, typing on a number pad or putting cards in order. Coins, achievements, buddies and themes keep it fun.

What’s free?

Six games are always free, plus a free Game of the Day. Full Unlock is one purchase with no subscription that opens every game.

Can I change the voice or speed?

Yes. Open Settings on the home screen and choose the voice and how fast it reads.

Grown-ups

Where can I see my child’s progress?

Tap Grown-Ups on the home screen and answer the grown-ups question. The Grown-Ups Dashboard shows level, answers, accuracy, rounds, streak and the skills to practice.

What can be bought?

Full Unlock (every game) and the Style Pack (themes) are one-time purchases; the Coin Chest is a bundle of in-game coins. Buying any of them, and Restore Purchases, goes through the grown-ups question first.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
