Playing

How does a round work?

Pick an operation and a fact family (for example × 7), or Mixed. You have one minute to answer as many facts as you can. The answer is checked as soon as it has enough digits, or tap Go. A missed fact comes back a few questions later, and the facts to practise are shown at the end.

How are stars earned?

For a one-minute round: 1 star for 10 right answers, 2 stars for 20 and 3 stars for 30. Shorter or longer rounds are scaled to match. Each fact family keeps its best round.

Can I change the round length?

Yes: 30 seconds, 1 minute or 2 minutes, in Settings.

What order are the fact families in?

The order most classrooms teach them: 0, 1, 2, 10, 5, 3, 4, 9, 11, 6, 7, 8, 12. Division runs from ÷ 1 to ÷ 12.

For grown-ups

What is free?

Addition and subtraction are free. A one-time purchase adds multiplication and division. There is no subscription.

How do I start over?

Settings > Reset Stars and Scores.
