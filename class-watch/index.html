Class Watch privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Class Watch is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Settings: class mode on/off, what to hide, presenter view, pause at the end
  • Lesson segments and the lesson list: video links, titles, start and end times and your notes (Pro)
  • The PIN lock as a salted SHA-256 hash, never the PIN itself (Pro)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Class Watch makes no network requests of its own and never downloads videos. The only request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Class Watch sends the key and a device label (such as "Class Watch on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • storage: Keeps the teacher's settings (class mode on/off, what to hide, presenter view), lesson segments, the lesson list (video links, titles, times and notes), and the PIN lock as a salted hash, on this computer.
  • https://www.youtube.com/*: The content script runs only on www.youtube.com. On video watch pages in class mode it adds CSS that hides comments, suggestions, end cards, chat, Shorts shelves and the search bar, and it pauses the page's own video at the end (or at a lesson segment's end) so the next video doesn't start. It never hides or skips ads, never clicks anything, and reads nothing but the video's time, the title and the address.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Class Watch isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
