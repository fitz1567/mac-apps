Access Desk privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Access Desk is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Page check: ignore rules you set; with Pro, a history of your checks (page address, title and the issues found, up to 200 checks)
  • History & reports: the “Prepared by” name you type on a report
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Access Desk never sends pages or results anywhere: every check runs in your browser, only on the tab where you clicked its button. No account, no server, no analytics. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Access Desk sends the key and Access Desk's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Lets Page check read the page in the current tab only when the user clicks Access Desk's toolbar button (or presses its shortcut) on that page. Pages are never read automatically.
  • scripting: Runs the accessibility checks in that tab, and draws the “Show me” outline and the Tab order overlay on top of it in a separate layer (the page itself is not changed).
  • storage: Keeps each tool's data on this computer: Page check's ignore rules and, with Pro, the saved checks (page address, title and the issues found) that History & reports lists; the “Prepared by” name for reports; and the Pro license.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Access Desk open in Chrome's side panel beside the page they are checking.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Access Desk isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
