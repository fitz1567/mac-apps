Label Export for Gmail™ privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Label Export for Gmail™ is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your export settings (columns, separator, which labels, split size)
  • Your Pro license key, if you buy Pro
  • In Firefox and Edge: the Google sign-in token, in memory only (session storage), until it expires after an hour, the browser closes or you sign out

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Label Export connects only to Google, to sign you in (accounts.google.com), read your labels and, only if you turn on message export, the headers of messages in the labels you choose (gmail.googleapis.com), and revoke access when you sign out (oauth2.googleapis.com); and to Gumroad to check a Pro license key. The CSV is made in your browser and saved on your computer; nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. Gmail data is never used for advertising and is never read by a person.

What it reads from Gmail

  • Labels (scope gmail.labels): each label's name, type, colour, visibility and its message, thread and unread counts. Read only; it never creates, renames or deletes a label.
  • Only if you choose "Allow message export" (scope gmail.metadata): for the labels you tick, each message's date, From, To, Cc, Subject, Gmail's short preview (snippet) and labels. Never message bodies or attachments, and it can't send, change or delete email.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Label Export's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to make the export you ask for, is never transferred to anyone, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a person.

Signing out

Sign out (in the popup or Settings) revokes Label Export's access at Google and forgets the token in your browser. You can also remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Trademarks

Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC. Label Export is an independent extension, not made or endorsed by Google.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Label Export for Gmail™ sends the key and Label Export for Gmail™'s Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • identity: Signs the user in with Google's own OAuth window (chrome.identity) to get a token for the Gmail API. Scope gmail.labels by default; gmail.metadata only if the user turns on message export. The token is never stored by the extension on disk and is revoked when the user signs out.
  • storage: Keeps the user's export settings (columns, file format, which labels) and, if they buy Pro, the license key, on this computer. In Firefox and Edge, the sign-in token is held in session storage (memory only) until it expires or the user signs out.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Label Export for Gmail™ isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
