Clock Steps support

Children learn to read an analog clock: pick the time on the clock, drag the hands to set a time, and match digital times to clocks. Five levels from o'clock to any minute, times read aloud and shown in words, stars for every level, and a parent gate. No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet.

Get it

  • Fire tablets: search for "Clock Steps" in the Amazon Appstore.
  • Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets: search for "Clock Steps" in the Galaxy Store.

Bigger text and colors

Open Settings in the app to choose the text size, light or dark colors, and high contrast. The app also follows your device's own font size (Settings → Display → Font size).

New device

Sign in to the same store account on the new device and download Clock Steps again from your library at no charge.

Refunds

  • Purchases are handled by Amazon. To ask for a refund, contact Amazon customer service (amazon.com → Your Account → Your Apps & Devices, or Help → Contact us).
  • Purchases are handled by Samsung. To ask for a refund, open Galaxy Store → Menu → Orders, or contact Samsung through the Galaxy Store help pages.
