Post Draft Pad privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Post Draft Pad is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your drafts and their folders
  • Your saved hashtag sets
  • Which previews you chose to show
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Post Draft Pad never posts anything, has no access to any website, and never sends your drafts anywhere. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Post Draft Pad sends the key and a device label (such as "Post Draft Pad on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: Shows the writing pad in Chrome's side panel, so the user can draft next to whatever page they have open.
  • storage: Keeps the user's drafts, folders, saved hashtag sets and preview choices on this computer.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Post Draft Pad isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
