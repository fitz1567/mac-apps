Speed Peek privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Speed Peek is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your settings
  • With Pro: a history of your measurements (the page address and title, the speed numbers and which fixes applied), up to 20 per page, to compare before and after
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Speed Peek reads only the page you click it on, from the performance records Chrome already keeps, and never sends anything about it anywhere. It makes no requests of its own (it doesn't call PageSpeed Insights or any other service). Reload and measure reloads the tab you clicked, once, when you ask. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Speed Peek sends the key and a device label (such as "Speed Peek on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Reads the speed measurements of the page in the current tab only when the user clicks the toolbar button (or uses its shortcut).
  • scripting: Runs a read-only function in that tab to read the browser's own performance records (paint, layout-shift, interaction and file timings). With Pro, it also draws a temporary outline around the element the user asks to see. The page is not otherwise changed.
  • storage: Keeps the user's settings, their Pro measurement history (page address, title and numbers, for before/after comparisons) and the Pro license on this computer.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Speed Peek isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
