PDF Stack privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

PDF Stack is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your settings (save to Downloads or ask where; last page-number, watermark and compress choices)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

PDF Stack never uploads your files. They are opened, shown and rebuilt inside the tab on your computer, and it works with no internet connection. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, PDF Stack sends the key and a device label (such as "PDF Stack on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • storage: Keeps the user's settings (how to save files, last page-number and watermark choices) and their Pro license key on this computer. The PDFs themselves are never stored; they stay in the open tab's memory.
  • optional:https://api.lemonsqueezy.com/*: Asked for only when the user activates a Pro license key, to check the key.
  • content_security_policy (wasm-unsafe-eval): Not a permission. The same as Chrome's default for extension pages, written out: the bundled PDF reader (pdf.js) decodes some image types (JPEG 2000, JBIG2) and color profiles with WebAssembly files that ship inside the extension. No code is fetched from anywhere.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

PDF Stack isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
