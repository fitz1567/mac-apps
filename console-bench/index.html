Console Bench

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Console Bench does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Console Bench (“the app”), an app for repairing game consoles, logging refurbished units for resale, and running a small repair shop, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Console Bench does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Refurb units you log: model, serial number, grade, purchase and sale details, test results, repairs, parts, time, listing notes and photos
  • Customers you add: name, phone, email and notes
  • Repair tickets: the device, serial number, problem, accessories, photos, the customer’s signature, diagnosis, quote, status history, payment and warranty
  • Parts you keep in stock: names, counts, costs, prices, suppliers and locations
  • Which repair guide steps you’ve ticked off
  • Your settings: shop details printed on documents, rates, tax, fees, currency, paper size and the app lock
  • A short summary for the Console Bench widgets (counts, ticket numbers with device names and statuses, unit titles; no customer details), kept in a container on your device that only the app and its widgets can read

It stays in Console Bench’s own storage on your device and is never sent to us. If iCloud is on for Console Bench, it is also kept in your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices; that copy belongs to your Apple Account and we can’t see it. It is removed if you delete the app (and from iCloud if you remove the app’s data in iCloud settings). Deleting a customer also deletes their tickets, photos and signatures.

Your customers’ information

If you use Shop mode, you are the one collecting your customers’ names, contact details and signatures, for your own records. Console Bench keeps them only on your devices and in your iCloud, and never sends them to us or anyone else. You can delete a customer and everything linked to them at any time, and turn on the Face ID or passcode lock in Settings.

Photos, camera and documents

Photos come from the system photo picker, which shares only the pictures you choose, or from the camera when you take one. They’re made smaller and saved without location data. PDFs, CSV files and status messages go only where you send or save them. Printing goes straight to your printer.

Widgets, Shortcuts and the App Store

The widgets and Shortcuts actions run on your device. The only network use is Apple’s: iCloud sync, if you have it on, and the App Store for the Pro purchase.

Purchases

Console Bench is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
