Fix It

Which consoles are covered?

52 consoles and controllers: Nintendo (NES to the Switch family, Game Boy, DS and 3DS), Sega (Master System to Dreamcast), PlayStation 1–5, PSP and Vita, Xbox to Series X|S, and their controllers. Pick one in Fix It, or search by name (regional names like Mega Drive work too).

Where do the guides come from?

They’re written for Console Bench from general repair knowledge. They aren’t copied from service manuals or other guides, and they’re general advice: models and board revisions differ, so check yours, and stop when a guide says the job needs a repair shop. Repairs are at your own risk.

Why do some guides ask me to confirm first?

Advanced repairs (soldering, power supplies, batteries) list their safety notes first and ask you to confirm you’ve read them before the steps appear.

Is Console Bench official?

No. Console Bench isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Sega, Sony or Microsoft. Their product names are used only to say which hardware a guide is for.

Refurb

How is profit worked out?

Profit = sale price + shipping the buyer paid − marketplace fees (the percentage on the whole order plus any fixed fee) − shipping you paid − what you paid for the unit − parts − other costs. Set your usual fee in Settings › Refurb defaults; check your marketplace’s current fees.

How do I export my units?

Refurb › the share button, or Settings › Export. You get a CSV file for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Shop

How do I get the customer’s signature?

Open a ticket and tap Customer Signature. They sign with a finger, Apple Pencil or the mouse. Your drop-off terms from Settings › Your shop are shown above it and printed on the intake receipt.

How do I send an invoice or a status update?

In a ticket, open Documents for the intake receipt, quote, invoice or status report as a PDF to print or share. Send a Status Update writes a short message you can send by Messages or email.

How does the warranty work?

Set the warranty days on the ticket (the default is in Settings). It starts when you mark the ticket Picked up, and the Tickets list shows repairs still under warranty.

Console Bench Pro

What’s free and what’s Pro?

Free: every repair guide, 10 refurb units and 3 shop tickets. Pro is one purchase: unlimited units and tickets and the parts inventory. No subscription. Family Sharing is on, and one purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Settings › Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
