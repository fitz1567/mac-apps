Need a hand?

Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn’t, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

Playing

How does a child choose?

By looking at a target for a moment (dwell), by a smile, raised eyebrows or a long blink, or with a switch in scan mode. A grown-up’s tap on a target always works too.

Which devices can follow the eyes?

iPhones and iPads with Face ID (a TrueDepth camera). On others, use switch scanning or touch.

How do I make it more accurate?

In the grown-ups’ settings, run the eye check: nine dots set the calibration, five more measure it and suggest a target size.

Grown-ups

What is the grown-ups question?

A multiplication you answer by typing the number, after holding the button. It comes before the settings, the purchase and any link out of the app.

What’s free?

Two games (Lantern Glow and Bubble Float), the eye check and every setting. One purchase with no subscription opens the other four. Family Sharing is on.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the grown-ups’ settings in the app (after the grown-ups question) and tap Restore purchase.
