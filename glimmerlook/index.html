Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Glimmerlook does not collect any data, from children or anyone else.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Glimmerlook (“the app”), calm play for children on iPad and iPhone, played with the eyes, head pointing, face gestures or a switch, made for the App Store Kids category, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Glimmerlook does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your iPhone or iPad

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • The grown-ups’ settings: input mode, how a choice is made, dwell and scan times, target size, sounds
  • The eye-check calibration: six numbers that adjust the pointer, not camera data
  • Whether all games are unlocked

It stays in the app’s own storage on your device (iOS’s standard app preferences) and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Face tracking stays on the device

In Eyes, Head and face-gesture modes, Glimmerlook uses the front (TrueDepth) camera through Apple’s ARKit to follow where the child looks, which way the head points and three face movements (a smile, raised eyebrows, a long blink). It reads only those few numbers, frame by frame, in memory, to move the pointer. No picture, video or face data is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and it is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose. Switch and Touch modes don’t use the camera.

Made for kids

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, chat, social features or third-party code, and makes no internet requests. The purchase, restore and the link to this policy are in the grown-ups’ settings, behind a grown-ups-only question.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

The app is made for children and collects no personal information from them or anyone else, so there is nothing to share, sell or disclose.

Purchases

Glimmerlook is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: help@fitztoppicks.com.
