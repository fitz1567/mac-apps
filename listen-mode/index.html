Listen Mode privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Listen Mode is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your speed and voice choices
  • With Pro: where you stopped in each article (page address, title and sentence number)
  • With Pro: the articles you saved to listen later (title, address and text)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Listen Mode never sends the pages you read anywhere. Speech uses the voices on your computer; if you choose one of Chrome's voices marked “online”, Chrome sends that text to Google to speak it. The only request Listen Mode makes itself is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Listen Mode sends the key and Listen Mode's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Reads the article in the tab only when the user clicks the toolbar button or presses its shortcut.
  • scripting: Adds the reader (the player, the sentence highlight and the text it reads) to that tab.
  • storage: Keeps the user's speed and voice choices, their place in articles (Pro) and the articles they saved to listen later (Pro) on this computer.
  • tts: Speaks the text with the speech voices installed on the user's computer (Chrome's text-to-speech API). Web pages can't use these voices from an extension's content script, so the extension's service worker speaks for the page.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Listen Mode isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
