Playing

How do I choose a grade?

The first time you open the app, tap a grade. To switch later, tap Change grade under the title on the home screen. Each grade keeps its own progress, coins and settings, so brothers and sisters can each use their own grade on one device.

How do I start?

Pick a buddy, then tap any game. There’s no account or sign-in.

What happens when I get one wrong?

You get a second try, then a step-by-step explanation of how to solve it.

Can questions be read aloud?

Yes. Reading aloud is on by default in grades 2–4 and can be turned on or off in Settings in every grade.

Rewards

How do stars, coins and levels work?

Every round earns stars, coins and XP. Coins buy buddies, color themes and power-ups in the Reward Shop, and there’s a free daily gift.

For parents

Where can I see progress?

Tap Grown-Ups (Parents in grades 6–8) on the home screen and answer the parent-gate question. The dashboard shows accuracy skill by skill for the grade that’s open.

Purchases

What’s free?

Six games in every grade are always free, plus a free game of the day.

What can I buy?

An unlock for one grade ($2.99), or All Grades 2–8 ($9.99) for every grade. There’s also an optional Style Pack and a Coin Chest. All are one-time purchases; there are no subscriptions or ads.

Why is there a question before I can buy?

Purchases are on a grown-ups screen behind a parent gate: a multiplication question written out in words, answered in numbers, so children don’t buy by accident.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the grown-ups screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases carry over to any device signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress & privacy

Does progress sync between devices?

Progress is saved on the device, separately for each grade. It doesn’t sync between devices and is removed if you delete the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry over.

What data does the app collect?

None. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
