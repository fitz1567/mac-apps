Privacy Policy

Learning Adventure: Grades 2–8
Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Learning Adventure: Grades 2–8 is an educational app with learning games for grades 2 through 8 (about ages 7–14). The app works offline and runs entirely on the user’s device. It only goes online through Apple: when a grown-up buys or restores an optional in-app purchase, or when the grown-ups’ screen shows Apple’s rating prompt or opens one of our other learning apps in the App Store.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information whatsoever.

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any identifiable data
  • No location data
  • No camera, microphone, or photo access
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • All progress, scores, and learning activities are saved locally on the device only, separately for each grade
  • Which in-app purchases have been bought is also saved on the device, so they keep working offline

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases: an unlock for each grade and All Grades 2–8 (one-time purchases), the Style Pack (one-time) and the Coin Chest (a bundle of in-game coins). All of them are on a grown-ups’ screen that children reach only by passing a parent gate: a multiplication question written out in words, answered in numbers.

  • Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store, using the Apple Account on the device. Apple processes the payment; we never see names, payment details, or any other personal information. The app only receives Apple’s confirmation of which item was bought.
  • Purchases can be restored with Restore Purchases on the same grown-ups’ screen.
  • The app has no ads. The grown-ups’ screen, behind the same parent gate, has a Rate This App button and links to our other learning apps: they show Apple’s rating prompt or open the App Store. There are no other links out of the app, and children can’t reach these without passing the gate.

Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This app is designed for students in grades 2–8 (about ages 7–14), including children under 13, and fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information, no parental consent is required. Any purchase is made by a grown-up, behind the parent gate, through Apple.

Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties because we do not collect any data.

Data Security

All information stays on the user’s device and is protected by the device’s built-in security features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
