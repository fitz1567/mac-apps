Recruiter Kit privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Recruiter Kit is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your saved Boolean searches and folders (titles, skills, locations, exclusions and the strings built from them) and the search you're working on
  • Your interview plans (questions, competencies, rating anchors, the role's saved search)
  • Candidate names, ratings, notes, recommendations and summaries you type (with Pro, every candidate; free keeps the current one)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Recruiter Kit never sends your searches, plans or candidate notes anywhere and never reads the pages you visit, including sourcing sites, video calls and applicant tracking systems: no account, no server, no analytics, no site access. When you click Search, it opens a Google or Bing results page in a new tab with your string, as if you had typed it. The only network request it makes itself is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Recruiter Kit sends the key and Recruiter Kit's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • storage: Keeps each tool's data on this computer: the user's saved Boolean searches, folders and current draft; their interview plans (questions, competencies, rating anchors, the linked saved search), candidate names, ratings, notes and recommendations.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Recruiter Kit open in Chrome's side panel, beside a video call, an applicant tracking system or a sourcing site. It never reads or changes that page.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Recruiter Kit isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
