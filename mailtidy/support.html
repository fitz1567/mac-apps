Getting started

How do I make an app password?

Gmail: turn on 2-Step Verification, then open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type “Mailtidy” and click Create. Yahoo: open Account security and choose Generate app password. iCloud Mail (two-factor authentication on): open account.apple.com › Sign-In and Security › App-Specific Passwords and create one; sign in with your full iCloud address. Paste the password into Mailtidy; spaces don’t matter. Work and school Google accounts, and those with Advanced Protection, can’t make app passwords.

Can I see it before connecting my mail?

Yes. On the welcome screen choose Try It with a Made-Up Inbox (or Mail › Try the Demo). Every screen works on made-up mail, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Does it work with Outlook.com or Hotmail?

Not yet: Outlook.com is coming in an update. Gmail, Yahoo Mail and iCloud Mail work now.

Cleaning up

What will it never touch?

Starred or flagged mail, your sent mail and drafts, senders you protect or Keep, mail waiting on your reply, and (on by default, in Settings › Auto-protect) banks and credit unions, government addresses, health care, payments and receipts, and people you have written to. All of these are checked again right before anything moves.

How do I undo a cleanup?

Click Undo on the result, choose Mail › Undo Last Cleanup (⌥⌘Z), use History, or click Undo in the notification after a scheduled cleanup. Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud empty Trash on their own after about 30 days, so undo before then.

How does scheduled cleanup work?

It’s off until you turn it on in Settings › Scheduled cleanup. Pick daily or weekly and the groups to clean. It runs while Mailtidy is open (and soon after you open it, if a run is due), then shows a notification with what moved and an Undo button.
