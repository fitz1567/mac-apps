Dead Link Scan privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Dead Link Scan is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your settings (ignore patterns, time limit)
  • The current scan's link list and results, only until the browser closes
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Dead Link Scan sends nothing to us or anyone else. To check a link it asks that link's own server for the address once (a HEAD request, or a GET that stops after the headers), with no cookies and no referrer, and only for sites you allowed. Pro's site crawl reads up to 50 pages of the site you're on, after reading its robots.txt. Checking a Pro license key contacts Lemon Squeezy when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Dead Link Scan sends the key and a device label (such as "Dead Link Scan on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Gives access to the current tab only when the user clicks the toolbar button, to read that page's links.
  • scripting: Runs a one-time function in that tab to list its links, images and files, and (when the user clicks “Show on page”) to outline the broken ones.
  • storage: Keeps the user's settings (ignore patterns, time limit), the license key, and the current scan for this browser session, on this computer.
  • http://*/*, https://*/* (optional): Never granted at install. When the user starts a check, Chrome asks for the scanned site only (e.g. https://example.com/*); links to other sites are checked only if the user then allows that exact list of sites. Each address gets one header-only request, without cookies.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Dead Link Scan isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
