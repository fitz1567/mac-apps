Reading Desk support

Read the web your way: hear articles aloud, easier fonts, speed reading, read later, clean print, recipe cards, RSS.

The 7 tools

  • Listen: Hear the article (or your selection) read aloud by your computer’s voices, each sentence highlighted as it’s read. Free: reads the article or a selection with sentence highlighting and auto-scroll, click any sentence to start there, 1×–1.5×, saved articles read aloud. Pro: 0.75×–3×, every voice with a pick per language, a listen-later queue, keyboard shortcuts, skipping citations, your place remembered.
  • Easy view: A dyslexia-friendly font, roomier spacing, a narrower column, warm tints and a line ruler, on the page you’re reading. Free: one-click easy view with Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic, the line ruler, the page put back exactly. Pro: every font, text size, letter/word/line spacing and column width, 7 tints, word anchors, presets, auto-apply on chosen sites.
  • Speed read: Speed read the article or your selection one word at a time, with smart pauses, a focus letter and time left. Free: 150–400 words a minute, one word at a time, the article or a selection, every pause and key, saved articles too. Pro: up to 1,000 wpm, 2–3 word chunks, a warm-up ramp, themes and text size, reading history with your speed, saved places.
  • Print & PDF: Print or save the page as a clean PDF: the article on its own, click to remove ads and menus, text size and images. Free: reader view and page view, click to remove with undo, text size, images and backgrounds on or off. Pro: removals remembered per site, one-click article only, font, page margins, links as footnotes, editable site rules.
  • Recipe card: Turn a recipe page into a clean card: check off ingredients and steps, print it, keep the screen on while you cook. Free: the clean card with checkboxes, print layout, keep screen on, the source credited and linked. Pro: scale servings with real fractions, US and metric, a recipe box with tags and notes, one shopping list from several recipes, exports.
  • Read later: Save the article as clean text with its images and read it later, even offline, in a calm reader; listen to it or speed read it. Free: 100 saved articles with images, the reader (font, size, width, light/sepia/dark), full-text search, read/unread. Pro: unlimited articles, tags and folders, highlights, export to Markdown, HTML or JSON, Pocket/Instapaper/Omnivore import.
  • Feeds: A private RSS, Atom and JSON Feed reader in its own tab: subscribe from any site, read offline, save items to Read later. Free: 25 feeds, folders, read/unread and stars, a refresh every 30 minutes, save any item to Read later. Pro: unlimited feeds, keyword hide/highlight rules, OPML import and export, starred export, search, refresh every 5–15 minutes.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Reading Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 7 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Reading Desk reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.

  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.
