Using Batchthread

A supplier says a lot is bad. What do I do?

Open Trace, search the supplier’s own lot code, and Batchthread shows every batch and customer it reached. Hold Affected Lots puts everything still in stock on hold.

Can I delete a mistake?

No, by design: void it with a reason. Its code stays retired and the audit log keeps the change.

Batchthread license

What’s free, and what does a key add?

Without a key you can keep 3 products and 25 lots with the trace on screen and backups. A key removes the limits and adds the recall report and CSV, batch record PDFs and lot labels, on up to 3 Macs. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Batchthread › Settings…, open the License tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Batchthread checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Batchthread menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
