Quillrescue privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Quillrescue is a Firefox add-on by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics or tracking of any kind, and no server of ours: what you save stays in Firefox on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Snapshots of the text you type in web form fields in normal windows (never in private windows), with the site, the page address and title, and the field's label. They are kept unencrypted in your Firefox profile until they age out (30 days, 90 with Pro) or you delete them (Settings › Delete every draft)
  • Your Quillrescue settings, the sites where it's off and the day it last cleared out old versions
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro, and the buyer email address Gumroad returns when it checks the key
  • Fingerprints (SHA-256 hashes, not the keys themselves) of license keys already activated in this browser, so entering a key again doesn't use another of its 3 places

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Quillrescue has no server. Your drafts stay in Firefox on this computer and are never sent anywhere. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Private windows

Firefox runs add-ons in private windows only if you allow it (about:addons › Quillrescue › Run in Private Windows). Even then, Quillrescue keeps nothing from a private window: it doesn't save what you type there or offer drafts back there, and its toolbar button can't turn a site on or off from one, so no site you visit privately is written down.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Quillrescue sends the key and Quillrescue's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent. Firefox asks you first, and lists it as optional data collection (“authentication information”): if you say no, nothing is sent and the free features keep working.

Permissions

  • storage: Keeps the snapshots of text the user typed (with the site, page address, page title and field label), the user's settings, the sites where it is off and the license, in the browser on this computer.
  • <all_urls>: The content script watches the text fields the user types in on any site (textareas, text inputs and rich editors; never password, card, one-time-code, hidden or login-form fields) and saves their text locally, then offers it back when the user returns to an empty field. It sends nothing anywhere.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Quillrescue isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
