The short version: Rowfill does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Rowfill (“the app”), a Mac app that fills a PDF form once for every row of a spreadsheet, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Rowfill does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your settings: how dates are read and written, and the last output choices (file-name pattern, merge and flatten)
  • The matching presets you save (field names, column names and fixed text), in Rowfill’s own folder on your Mac
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

Settings are in Rowfill’s preferences, presets in its Application Support folder, and the license key in your Keychain. The form and the spreadsheet are read into memory while you work; the filled PDFs, merged PDF, fillable copy and reports are new files, written only where you choose, and never over a file that’s already there.

Your files

Rowfill runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, so it can open only the files and folders you pick or drop on it. It never changes the form or the spreadsheet you open. A PDF’s password, if it has one, is used only to open it and isn’t saved.

Apple Intelligence

Suggest Matches uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac: the names of the form’s blank fields, the words printed next to them, their kinds and your sheet’s column names are read by Apple’s on-device model, never a row of your data, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Network

Rowfill is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com. Neither sends anything about your forms or data.

Updates and license checks

Rowfill, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Rowfill contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again about once a week. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Rowfill is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
