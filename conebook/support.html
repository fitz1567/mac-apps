Using Conebook

Where is my logbook saved?

In a file on your Mac, saved as you work, with your photos beside it. File › Back Up saves everything in one .conebook file. Settings › General › Show Data Folder in Finder shows it.

I deleted something by mistake.

Choose Edit › Undo right away: it puts it back where it was.

Is the unity formula a safety test?

No. It is a calculation from the material analyses you enter. Only a lab test of the fired ware can tell you about leaching.

Does Conebook take payments from members?

No. Studio records charges and payments (cash, check, a card taken on your own reader, a transfer) and prints statements; it never handles money or card numbers.

Conebook license

What’s free, and what does a key add?

Without a key: the firing log and loads with photos, kilns and schedules, 8 glaze recipes with the unity formula, 10 test tiles, PDFs and backups, for good. A Pro key ($29) adds unlimited recipes and tiles, the blend generator, the Stull chart, tile labels, versions and CSV. A Studio key ($79) adds members, fee rules, the ledger and statements. Each is a one-time purchase on Gumroad for up to 3 Macs, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Conebook › Settings…, open the Pro and Studio tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Conebook checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Conebook menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
