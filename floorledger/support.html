Using Floorledger

Where are my records saved?

In the app’s folder on your Mac, saved as you work, one folder per module. Settings › General › Show Data Folder in Finder shows it.

Can I bring in what I kept in the separate shop apps?

Yes: Shop › Import from Old Apps finds Handsgrid, Calshelf, Wrenchweek, Tallyhatch, Tagroute, Quotebench and Offbench on this Mac, and Recordbench, Tackroll, Weighshelf, Lockbinder and Hazshelf files in a folder you choose. The old files are copied and only read.

How do quotes and outside processing work together?

When a quote is won, Send to Outside Processing hands its outside operations to the Outside Processing module, which shows them for a check before adding them as planned send-outs. Update from Outside Processing brings each processor’s approval and on-time rate into your quotes.

Can I hide the modules I don’t use?

Yes: Settings › Modules. A module that’s off is hidden with its menu; its records are kept.

Floorledger license

What’s free, and what does a key add?

Without a key you can use the skills matrix for up to 8 people and the shared shop records, for as long as you like. A key opens all twelve modules with no limits, on up to 3 Macs. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Floorledger › Settings…, open the Pro tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Floorledger checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Floorledger menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
