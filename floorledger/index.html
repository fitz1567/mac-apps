Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Floorledger does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Floorledger (“the app”), a Mac app: people, equipment, jobs and safety records for small shops, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Floorledger does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your shop records: the shop profile and logo, and the shared people, places, equipment and vendors
  • Each module’s records: skills and certificates; injury and illness cases (including the private Form 301 details, which can be locked with a passphrase); weld procedures and welder records; calibration history and certificates; maintenance tasks and work orders; tool crib loans and badges; extinguisher inspections; refrigerant service records; lockout procedures, photos and training; quotes, customers and rates; outside processing send-outs and the cert files you attach; the chemical list and the SDS PDFs you add
  • Copies of what you import from the old shop apps (Imported) and of a module’s records an import replaced (Replaced)
  • Your settings: which modules are on, each module’s preferences, and the tool crib counter PIN (kept as a salted hash, not the PIN itself)
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

Records are in Floorledger’s Application Support folder (named ShopSuite; one folder per module, with daily backups where a module makes them), settings in its preferences, and the license key in your Keychain. Exports, PDFs, binders and backups are new files, written only where you choose. If you choose a shop folder (maintenance, tool crib, outside processing, a shared refrigerant records package), that module also writes its records there, so your other Macs can read them; that folder is yours, on your drive, server or cloud storage.

Your files

Floorledger runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, so it can open and save only where you choose. Your records are read and written on your Mac (and in a shop folder you choose) and never uploaded or sent to us. SDS PDFs are read on your Mac (with Apple’s text recognition for scans); nothing is sent to a server to read them.

Network

Floorledger is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com (a new version downloads from GitHub). Neither sends anything about your records. Find SDS opens a web search in your browser and Email Quote or Ask the Supplier opens your own mail app; Floorledger itself downloads nothing. To import, it reads (never changes) the old shop apps’ own folders, after macOS asks you.

Notifications

Reminders (certificates, calibrations, PM tasks, inspections, follow-ups, parts due back, chemical expiry) are local notifications scheduled on your Mac, only for the modules where you turn them on.

Updates and license checks

Floorledger, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from GitHub, where this app’s downloads are kept, only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Floorledger contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Floorledger is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
