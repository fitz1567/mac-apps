Creator Desk support

For video creators: a private teleprompter under your camera, and a thumbnail and title preview in a mock feed.

The 2 tools

  • Teleprompter: Write or paste your script, then read it from a teleprompter window under your camera that scrolls at your speed. Free: one script, steady scrolling at your words per minute, reading time, text size, colours, reading line, countdown, keyboard and mouse wheel, [PAUSE] and [CUE] marks. Pro: unlimited scripts in folders with search, import and export, mirror and upside-down, cue stops and timed pauses, voice pacing, pedal and remote keys, keep on top.
  • Thumbnail test: See your thumbnail and title in a mock video feed before you publish: where the title gets cut off, size checks and how it reads small. Free: one thumbnail and title in a home grid and search results, light and dark, title fit, image size checks, small-size previews, brightness hints. Pro: up next and mobile views, 2–3 versions side by side with shuffle, your own comparison thumbnails, the squint test, saved tests, PNG export.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Creator Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Creator Desk's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $15 once, not a subscription.
  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 2 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Creator Desk reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.
