Label Printer Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Label Printer Studio does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Label Printer Studio (“the app”), a Mac app that makes price tags, barcode labels and other labels from a spreadsheet and prints them through the Mac’s print system, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Label Printer Studio does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac only:

  • Your current label design, and a copy of the rows of the spreadsheet you imported last, so your labels are there next time
  • The templates you save under My Templates

It stays in Label Printer Studio’s own storage on your Mac and is never sent to us. Removing the spreadsheet in the app (File › Remove Spreadsheet) deletes its copy. Deleting the app and its container (in your user Library, under Containers) removes the rest.

Your spreadsheets

Label Printer Studio opens only the CSV or Excel files you choose or drop on its window, and reads them on your Mac. Nothing in them is sent anywhere.

Printing and saving

Labels go to your printer through the Mac’s own print dialog, and PDFs and PNGs are saved only where you choose. The app has no network access of its own; the App Store handles the Pro purchase.

Purchases

Label Printer Studio is sold through Apple’s Mac App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
