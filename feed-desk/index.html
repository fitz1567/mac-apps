Feed Desk privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Feed Desk is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • The feeds you subscribe to, your folders and settings
  • Items fetched from those feeds (title, link, text), read/unread and starred state
  • Your keyword rules (Pro)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Feed Desk fetches only the feeds you subscribe to, directly from each site, without cookies. Nothing is sent to the developer. Checking a Pro license key contacts Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Feed Desk sends the key and Feed Desk's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • storage: Keeps the user's subscriptions, folders, settings and fetched feed items on this computer.
  • unlimitedStorage: Fetched items (the text of each post) are kept for offline reading; without it a few dozen feeds would hit Chrome's 10 MB storage limit and old items would be lost.
  • alarms: Checks subscribed feeds for new items on a schedule (every 30 minutes, or 5–15 minutes with Pro).
  • activeTab: When the user clicks the toolbar button, reads the current tab to find the feed links that page advertises.
  • scripting: Runs a one-time function in that tab to list its <link rel="alternate"> feed links.
  • https://*/*: Optional and never granted at install: when the user subscribes to a feed, Feed Desk asks for access to that one feed's site only (e.g. https://example.com/*), to fetch the feed.
  • http://*/*: Optional, same as above, for feeds on sites that don't use https.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Feed Desk isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
