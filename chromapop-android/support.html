ChromaPop support

Tap every dot that matches the center color before time runs out. ChromaPop is a fast, colorful reaction game that is easy to learn and hard to put down. Five ways to play, a new Daily Challenge every day, four power-ups, Colorblind Mode with a symbol on every color, and four dot themes. Every mode is included. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.

Get it

  • Fire tablets: search for "ChromaPop" in the Amazon Appstore.

How to play

Tap PLAY for a Classic game: pop every dot that matches the color in the center before time runs out. A wrong tap costs a life. Time Attack, Zen and Hardcore are on the home screen, and the Daily Challenge card plays today's boards. Every mode and dot theme is included; there is nothing to buy inside the game.

Colorblind Mode, themes and sound

The switches on the home screen turn sound and Colorblind Mode on or off. Colorblind Mode adds a symbol to every color, with palettes for red-green color blindness. DOT THEMES picks Neon, Candy, Bubble or Gem.

Best scores

BEST SCORES on the home screen lists your best score in each mode and your Daily Challenge streak. They are kept on the tablet you play on; there are no accounts and no online leaderboards.

Back button

The tablet's Back button closes an open sheet, or returns to the home screen from a game.

New device

Sign in to the same store account on the new device and download ChromaPop again from your library at no charge.

Refunds

Something not working?

Email help@fitztoppicks.com with what happened, your device model and the app version (your device's Settings app shows it under Apps → ChromaPop).
