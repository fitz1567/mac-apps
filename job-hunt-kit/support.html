Job Hunt Kit support

For job seekers: save job posts to a private tracker, match your resume's keywords, compare the pay and prep interview stories.

The 4 tools

  • Job tracker: Save any job post in one click, with its description, and track your applications on a private board. Free: 25 saved jobs with their descriptions, board and table, notes, contacts, dates and history. Pro: unlimited jobs, follow-up reminders, stats (response rate, time to an answer), tags, CSV export and import.
  • Pay converter: Finds the pay in a job post and shows it by the hour, week, month and year, with your own hours. Free: reads the pay from any job post, hourly/weekly/monthly/yearly with your hours, fix it by hand, copy, add it to the saved job. Pro: take-home estimate, other currencies at your rates, compare up to 5 jobs with commute and benefits, CSV.
  • Resume keywords: Compares a job post (or a saved job) with your resume: a match score, the keywords you have and the ones you’re missing. Free: one resume, match score, matched keywords and the top 8 missing, check any saved job. Pro: every missing keyword, up to 5 resumes, saved comparisons with CSV export.
  • Interview stories: Your career stories in STAR form, matched to 80 interview questions and to a saved job’s requirements, with timed practice. Free: 5 stories, the question library with matches and gaps, a saved job’s requirements matched to your stories, practice with a timer. Pro: unlimited stories, 1- and 3-minute versions, prep lists and a one-page cheat sheet, practice progress, exports.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Job Hunt Kit. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.
