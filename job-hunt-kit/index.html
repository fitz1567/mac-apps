Job Hunt Kit privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Job Hunt Kit is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • The job posts you save (title, company, location, pay, link and a copy of the description) and what you add: status and dates, notes, contacts, tags, follow-up dates
  • Your resume text (with Pro, up to 5 named resumes), each saved job's keyword match score, and with Pro a history of the jobs you compared (title, link, score, keywords)
  • Your hours, tax-rate and currency settings and, with Pro, up to 5 jobs to compare (title, link, pay, commute cost, benefits value)
  • Your interview stories (headline, role, company, STAR text, numbers, tags), questions you add, prep lists and practice ratings
  • Each tool's settings, and your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Job Hunt Kit never sends your jobs, resume, pay numbers or stories anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics, no exchange-rate lookups. It reads a job post only when you open one of its tools on it, and it never fills in or submits anything on job sites. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Job Hunt Kit sends the key and Job Hunt Kit's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Lets the Job tracker, Pay converter and Resume keywords read the job post in the tab where the user clicked the toolbar button (or pressed its shortcut), and only that tab, only then.
  • scripting: When the user opens one of those tools on a job post, runs one function in that tab that reads the post: its title, company, location, pay and description (from the page's job-posting data, the selected text or the visible text). It changes nothing on the page.
  • storage: Keeps each tool's data on this computer: the jobs the user saves (with notes, contacts, dates and a copy of the description), their resume text and saved comparisons, their hours and pay settings and saved jobs to compare, and their interview stories, questions, prep lists and practice ratings.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Job Hunt Kit open in Chrome's side panel, next to a job post or an application form, or while they practice interview answers.
  • alarms: Wakes the extension at the follow-up date the user set on a saved job (Pro), so the reminder can be shown. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • notifications (optional): Never granted at install. Asked only when a Pro user first sets a follow-up date on a saved job; shows that reminder (e.g. "Follow up: Data Analyst at Acme"). No other notifications.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Job Hunt Kit isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
