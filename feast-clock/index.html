The short version: Feast Clock does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Feast Clock (“the app”), a big-meal oven and cooking planner for iPhone and iPad, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Feast Clock does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your iPhone or iPad

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Your meals: their names, dinner times, dishes (temperatures, times, oven room, notes) and the steps you ticked off
  • Your kitchen: ovens and racks, burners, slow cookers, air fryers and preheat time
  • Your settings: temperature and weight units, which meal is open, whether alarms are on, and whether Pro is unlocked

It stays in Feast Clock’s own storage on your device and is never sent to us. Feast Clock doesn’t sync it anywhere; your device’s own backups may include it, as they do for other apps. It is removed if you delete the app.

Alarms, notifications and the Live Activity

When you tap Set alarms for each step, iOS asks whether Feast Clock may set alarms. The alarms, the time-sensitive notifications used instead if you don’t allow alarms, and the Live Activity on the Lock Screen are all set on your device. Nothing goes through a server.

The turkey calculator

The thawing and roasting times come from the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service and are built into the app. Feast Clock doesn’t look anything up online.

Purchases

Feast Clock is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
