Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Throwline does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Throwline (“the app”), a projector, screen and seating planner for iPhone and iPad, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Throwline does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your iPhone or iPad

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Your rooms: size, screen, projector, mount and seat rows
  • Your projector models and their notes
  • Your choice of feet and inches or metres

Rooms and projectors are saved in Throwline’s Application Support folder on your device, the units setting in its preferences. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Works offline

Every calculation and drawing is made on your device. Throwline makes no internet requests.

Exports

The one-page PDF is shared or saved only where you choose.

Purchases

Throwline is a paid app through Apple’s App Store, with no in-app purchases. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: help@fitztoppicks.com.
