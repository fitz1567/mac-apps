The short version: Sheetbreak does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Sheetbreak (“the app”), a Mac app that splits a construction plan set PDF into one PDF per sheet, named from each sheet’s title block, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Sheetbreak does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your settings: the file-name preset and options, whether to read scanned pages with text recognition, and which sheet index files to write
  • The title-block box you used for each set (by the set’s file name), so the next version of a set opens with the same box
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

Settings and the remembered boxes are in Sheetbreak’s preferences on this Mac and the license key in your Keychain. The set you open is read into memory and never changed or moved; the sheet PDFs and the index are new files, in a new folder inside the one you choose.

Your files

Sheetbreak runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, so it can open only the PDFs you choose or drop on it, and save only in the folder you choose. Reading the title blocks, including text recognition for scanned pages, happens on your Mac; no page is uploaded anywhere.

Network

Sheetbreak is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID), checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com and the list of our other Mac apps, downloaded from fitztoppicks.com when you open it (at most once a day; the same file for everyone). None of them sends anything about your plan sets.

Updates and license checks

Sheetbreak, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Sheetbreak contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again about once a week. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Sheetbreak is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
