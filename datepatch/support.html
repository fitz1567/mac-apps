Using Datepatch

Why fix dates before importing?

Photos reads a picture’s date when you import it. Scans carry the day they were scanned, so a family archive lands on one afternoon. Fixing the files first means Photos sorts them by when they were taken, in scan order.

Which files work?

JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and PNG are dated inside the file. RAW files get an .xmp sidecar with the same name. Datepatch works on the photos at the top of the folder you open.

Does it change the picture?

No. JPEG, HEIC and TIFF are copied with new dates and no re-encoding, and every written photo is checked byte for byte against the original’s image data before it’s kept. A PNG is written again from its own pixels (PNG is lossless) and checked the same way.

What if I get it wrong?

By default Datepatch writes dated copies and leaves the originals alone, so just delete the copies. If you change the originals, Undo History puts the batch back as it was.

What about photos already in Photos?

Datepatch doesn’t touch your Photos library. Delete the undated ones from Photos, then import the dated copies; or use Photos’ own Adjust Date and Time for a few.

Datepatch Pro

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Everything works without a key, 25 photos per batch. Pro writes every photo in a batch. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Datepatch › Settings…, open the Pro tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Datepatch checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Datepatch menu. Updates are free.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
