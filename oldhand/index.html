Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Oldhand does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Oldhand (“the app”), a reading desk for old German handwriting (Kurrent and Sütterlin) for iPhone and iPad, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Oldhand does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your iPhone or iPad

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Your documents: the photo you add, its title, your line-by-line transcription and notes
  • Your settings
  • Whether the unlock is bought

Documents are saved in Oldhand’s Application Support folder on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you take a photo of a document, and photos you pick are read only when you add them. Photos: not collected. They are kept in the app, not in your photo library, and never uploaded.

No cloud reading

Oldhand doesn’t send your documents to any reading or AI service. The letter key and word list are built into the app.

Exports

Text and PDF exports are shared or saved only where you choose.

Purchases

Oldhand is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: help@fitztoppicks.com.
