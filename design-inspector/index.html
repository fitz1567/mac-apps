Design Inspector privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Design Inspector is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Each tool's settings (color format, ruler units and grid, AA or AAA target, page matching, test text mode and skip-lists, copy format)
  • With Pro: the CSS snippets you save (selector, CSS, Tailwind classes, page title and link)
  • With Pro: ruler guides you place, saved per site (the site's address and each guide's position)
  • With Pro: your brand palette for contrast checks, and the vision type you chose for each site (the site's name only)
  • The colors you pick (the last 20) and your palettes (names, notes and colors)
  • Saved font pairings and favorites (Pro), and the names of fonts installed on this computer if you allow listing them
  • The mockup images you add, their names and where you placed them on each page (page addresses), and (Pro) the sites you chose to restore overlays on
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Design Inspector never sends the pages you check, their styles, your colors, your mockups or anything else to us or anyone else: no account, no analytics. Everything runs in your browser. When you try a Google Font on a page, that page loads the font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), without the page address. Checking a Pro license key contacts Gumroad when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Design Inspector sends the key and Design Inspector's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Gives Design Inspector access to the current tab only when the user clicks its toolbar button (or presses its shortcut), so the on-page tools (Styles, Measure, Mockup overlay, Contrast, Color blindness, Try fonts, Long text) can work on that one page. With Pro, Measure's zoom loupe also takes a picture of that visible tab to magnify it; the picture is never saved or sent.
  • scripting: Adds the chosen tool to that tab after the user starts it: the inspector, ruler or contrast checker overlay (read-only, removed with Esc), the mockup overlay, a color filter for the color-blindness view, a stylesheet with the fonts being tried, or the test text (put back with Restore). Nothing runs on a page before the user asks.
  • storage: Keeps each tool's settings and the user's own work on this computer: saved CSS snippets, ruler guides per site, the contrast target and brand palette, the vision type per site, picked colors and palettes, font pairings and favorites, mockup images and their positions, and the Pro license.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Design Inspector open in Chrome's side panel next to the page, for example to pick colors from the screen or keep a palette at hand while working.
  • unlimitedStorage: Mockups (Mockup overlay) are full-page design exports, often 2–10 MB each as PNG; without it Chrome caps the extension's storage at 10 MB, which one or two mockups fill. Images stay on this computer and can be deleted in settings.
  • http://*/*, https://*/* (optional): Never granted at install. Asked for one site at a time, only by a Pro user who turns on Mockup overlay's “Bring it back after a reload” for that site, so the overlay can reappear after the page reloads. Turning it off gives the access back; it's also listed and removable on the settings page.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Design Inspector isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
