Design Inspector support

Design checks on the page you're building: styles, measuring, mockup overlay, contrast, color blindness, colors, fonts.

The 8 tools

  • Styles: Hover any element for its fonts, colors, spacing and CSS; click to pin it and copy clean CSS. Free: the styles card for any element, clean CSS for one element at a time, the page’s top colors and fonts, hex/rgb/hsl. Pro: Tailwind classes, every color and font on the page with CSS-variable and JSON export, distances between elements, saved snippets.
  • Measure: Measure the page in CSS pixels: drag a box, hover for padding and margin, hold Alt for the space between elements. Free: drag measuring with a live readout, the hover box model and the space between two elements. Pro: guides kept per site, snapping to element edges, a column and baseline grid, px/rem/% units, copy as text, a zoom loupe with the pixel color.
  • Mockup overlay: Lay your design mockup over the live page: opacity, 1px nudges, @2x scale and difference mode. Free: one mockup per page with opacity, offset, scale, difference mode and lock, remembered per page. Pro: several named mockups per site, bring the overlay back after a reload, grid and rulers, invert and hide shortcuts.
  • Contrast: Click any text for its WCAG contrast ratio, AA and AAA, and the nearest colors that pass. Free: the ratio with AA and AAA for any text, one suggested fix, the eyedropper, and how many text elements on the page fail. Pro: every failing element listed worst first and marked on the page, CSV, fixes in every direction, your brand palette checked.
  • Color blindness: See the page as people with color blindness see it, and check that two colors stay distinguishable. Free: protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and achromatopsia on any page, one at a time. Pro: weak color vision with a severity slider, blur and cataract, split view, the before/after shortcut, a vision type per site, the color pair check.
  • Colors & palettes: Pick any color on screen and keep it: HEX, RGB, HSL and OKLCH, a color history and palettes. Free: pick or paste colors, copy in four formats, the last 20 colors and one palette of 10. Pro: unlimited palettes with notes, OKLCH scales 50–950, harmonies, contrast between any two colors, export to CSS, SCSS, Tailwind, design tokens and .ase.
  • Try fonts: Try Google Fonts and your installed fonts on the page: headings, body text, size and weight, reset in one click. Free: any Google Font or installed font on headings and body text, size and weight, exact reset. Pro: saved pairings with A/B compare on the page, fonts on a clicked element, line height, letter spacing, type scales, favorites, CSS export.
  • Long text: Swap the page’s text for longer, accented test text to see what breaks before translation; restore in one click. Free: accented, bracketed text 30–50% longer on the page, text added later too, one-click restore. Pro: right-to-left and CJK full-width modes, your own expansion amount, outlines on text that no longer fits with a report, skip-lists.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Design Inspector. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.
