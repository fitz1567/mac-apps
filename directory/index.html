Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Directory does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Directory (“the app”), a file manager for the Mac that shows your files as a map, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Directory does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your settings, pinned places, recent folders, saved searches and workspaces
  • Folder sizes and map layouts it has measured, so the map opens quickly
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

It stays in Directory’s own storage on your Mac and is never sent to us.

Your files

Directory shows, previews and changes only the files and folders you ask it to. Nothing about your files is ever sent anywhere; the map, folder sizes, checksums, Git badges, duplicates and archives are all worked out on your Mac.

Ask

What you type into Ask is read on your Mac: by Apple’s on-device model on macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence on (Apple’s model runs on the Mac itself, not in the cloud), or by Directory’s built-in reader. Nothing you ask, and nothing about your files, leaves your Mac.

Search

Searches use Spotlight, the search built into macOS, on your Mac.

Network

Directory is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID), checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com and the list of our other Mac apps, downloaded from fitztoppicks.com when you open it (at most once a day; the same file for everyone). None of them sends anything about your files.

Updates and license checks

Directory, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Directory contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Directory is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
