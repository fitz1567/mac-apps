Fake Data Kit support

Make realistic, clearly fake test data offline: CSV, JSON or SQL, seeded and repeatable. Safe test emails, phones and cards.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Fake Data Kit. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Fake Data Kit's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $9 once, not a subscription.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Fake Data Kit reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email k.fitzgerald310@gmail.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email k.fitzgerald310@gmail.com with your order number.
