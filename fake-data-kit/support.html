Make realistic, clearly fake test data offline: CSV, JSON or SQL, seeded and repeatable. Safe test emails, phones and cards.

Install

Add it from the Chrome Web Store, then pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and the pin next to Fake Data Kit.

Pro

  • Buy: open Fake Data Kit's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $9 once, not a subscription.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Fake Data Kit reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 browsers. To move it, click Deactivate on this browser on the old one first.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Lemon Squeezy", or use the "My orders" link in your receipt.
