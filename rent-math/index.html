Rent Math privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Rent Math is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • The deal you're working on and your saved deals (prices, loan terms, rents, costs, and with Pro your notes and listing links)
  • Your starting numbers for new deals
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Rent Math never sends your deals anywhere and never reads listing pages on its own. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Rent Math sends the key and a device label (such as "Rent Math on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: Shows the calculator in Chrome's side panel, next to the listing or spreadsheet the user is looking at.
  • storage: Keeps the deal being worked on, the user's saved deals (numbers, notes, links) and their starting numbers on this computer.
  • activeTab: When the user clicks "Use selected price" after clicking the toolbar button, lets the extension read the text the user selected in that one tab. Pages are never read automatically.
  • scripting: Runs one function in that tab, only on that click, that returns the selected text (for example "$425,000") so it can be used as the price. It reads nothing else and changes nothing.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Rent Math isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
