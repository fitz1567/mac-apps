Effective November 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Driftpane does not collect your data; if you set a place for the weather, only its approximate coordinates go to MET Norway for the forecast.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Driftpane (“the app”), a Mac app that draws live 3D scenes on your desktop, behind your icons, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Apart from the license and update checks described below, one thing leaves your Mac, and only if you set a place for the weather: that place’s approximate coordinates (the coordinates Apple’s geocoder gives for the city or ZIP code you typed, or your position from Location Services rounded to about a kilometer), sent to MET Norway (api.met.no) with each forecast request so it can return the forecast for that spot. They aren’t linked to you, used for tracking or advertising, or sent anywhere else. When you type a place, Apple’s geocoder receives the text to find it. Driftpane has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your settings: the scene you picked, each scene’s options, Shuffle Daily, power, quality and idle-scene choices
  • The place for the weather: the city or ZIP code you typed and its coordinates, or your position from Location Services rounded to about a kilometer
  • The last weather forecast, so scenes keep the weather for a while offline
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

Your settings and place are in Driftpane’s preferences, the last forecast is in its Caches folder (both inside its own container in your user Library, under Containers) and the license key is in your Keychain. None of it is sent to us. Clearing the place in Settings › Weather removes it; deleting the app and its container removes the rest.

Your location (optional)

Driftpane works without a place: scenes then use fair weather and the sky for your time zone. For the real weather and sky, type a city or ZIP code in Settings › Weather, which Apple’s geocoder turns into coordinates (no permission needed), or turn on Use my location, which asks macOS for Location Services. Driftpane rounds your position to about a kilometer. It works out the sun and moon on your Mac, and only the coordinates go to MET Norway to ask for the forecast (below). You can turn it off, or clear the place, at any time.

Weather

While a scene that shows weather is on screen and a place is set, Driftpane asks MET Norway (api.met.no) for the forecast at that place, at most once an hour. The request carries only the coordinates, cut to 4 decimal places, and the app’s name and version. Weather data: MET Norway, CC BY 4.0. Earth from Orbit uses NASA’s Blue Marble and Black Marble imagery (NASA Earth Observatory), bundled with the app; it doesn’t download anything.

Network

Driftpane is sold directly through Gumroad. Its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID), checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com, the MET Norway forecast described above, and Apple’s geocoder when you type a place. None of them sends anything about what’s on your screen or which scenes you watch. No analytics.

Updates and license checks

Driftpane, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Driftpane contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Driftpane is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
