Commit Builder for Git privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Commit Builder for Git is a Microsoft Edge extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your settings, recent scopes and unfinished messages, in your browser only
  • With Pro: your repo profiles, commitlint rules, label and branch maps, ticket rules, type kit, templates, co-author book (names and emails you type) and your own GitHub Enterprise or GitLab hosts
  • With Pro: the last 200 messages you copied or filled (never from private windows)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro, and the buyer email address Gumroad returns when it checks the key (shown as “Licensed to …”)
  • Fingerprints (SHA-256 hashes, not the keys themselves) of license keys already activated in this browser, so entering a key again doesn’t use another of its 3 places

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

None for your messages: Commit Builder never sends anything you write, or anything about the pages you open it on, anywhere. The only request it can make is the Pro license check: when you click Activate, and about every two weeks after, it sends your license key and the product ID to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com).

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Commit Builder for Git sends the key and Commit Builder for Git's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: When you open Commit Builder on a pull request page, it reads that page's title, branch and labels to suggest a message, and fills the merge box when you click Fill on Page. It can't see other tabs, or any page you haven't opened it on.
  • scripting: Runs its two small functions (read the pull request; fill the message box) in the tab you opened it on, only when you use it.
  • storage: Saves your settings, recent scopes and unfinished messages in your browser, and with Pro your profiles, rules, templates, co-author book and message history, plus the Pro license.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/* (optional): Never granted at install. Asked for only when you click Activate on a Pro license key, to check the key with Gumroad (the store that sold it), and used again about every two weeks to recheck it.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Commit Builder for Git isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
