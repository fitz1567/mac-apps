Using Kennelroll

Where are my records saved?

In a file on your Mac, saved as you work. Settings › Data › Show Data Folder in Finder shows it.

How do I back up?

Kennelroll makes a backup each day you open it. File › Back Up… saves one file with everything, photos included; keep a copy on another drive. File › Restore from Backup… brings one back.

I deleted something by mistake.

Choose Edit › Undo right away: it puts it back.

Does Kennelroll know which vaccinations a dog needs?

No. It checks the dates you enter against your own list in Settings › Vaccinations. It makes no compliance, licensing or veterinary claims.

Kennelroll license

What’s free, and what does a key add?

Without a key you can keep up to 10 dogs, with the kennel board, the run sheet, vaccination tracking and every export and backup, for as long as you like. A key adds unlimited dogs, report cards, the printed feeding and medicine checklist, the records letter, reminders and spreadsheet import, on up to 2 Macs. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Kennelroll › Settings…, open the Pro tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Kennelroll checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Kennelroll menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
