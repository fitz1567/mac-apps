Need a hand?

Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn’t, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

Binders and items

How do I add an item?

Open a binder and tap an empty pocket (+). Choose Scan with Camera to find the edges and straighten the photo, or Choose Photo. Scanning two pages fills the front and the back.

How do I rearrange items?

Touch and hold an item, then drag it onto another pocket: an empty pocket takes it, a full one swaps. To move it to another binder, use the item’s menu. VoiceOver users have Move actions too.

Can I change the number of pockets?

Yes, any time: 4, 9, 12 or 18 per page in the binder’s settings. Items keep their order and the pages reflow. Gaps stay until you choose Close Gaps.

Why do I see two pages?

On iPad, larger iPhones in landscape and other wide screens, the binder opens as a two-page spread, and an item’s details open on the facing page. Upright on a phone, you see one page at a time.

Sync and export

Does my collection sync?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when Sync with iCloud is on in Settings and your devices use the same Apple Account. Give it a minute on a new device.

How do I export?

Open a binder, tap the ⋯ menu and choose Export PDF or CSV: a PDF of every page with photos in place, then a list, or a CSV. The same screen exports the whole collection as CSV.

Binder Spread Pro

What’s free and what does Pro add?

Free keeps up to 100 items with every feature. Pro is one purchase with no subscription that removes the limit. Nothing is hidden or deleted at 100. Family Sharing is on.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
