Using Cutwright

⌘X and ⌘V don’t do anything.

Open Setup in Cutwright and switch it on under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. If it still doesn’t react after an update, remove Cutwright from the Accessibility list with the minus button and add it back.

The right-click menu doesn’t show Cutwright’s items.

In Setup, click Open Extension Settings and switch Cutwright on under Finder extensions. Still missing? Hold Option, right-click Finder in the Dock and choose Relaunch.

Can it lose my files?

No: Cutwright never deletes anything. Cut and Paste uses Finder’s own Move Item Here (⌘Z in Finder moves them back), a taken name gets “ 2” instead of replacing a file, and Undo History takes back new files, copies, renames and unblocks.

Cutwright license

Is there a free trial?

Yes: every feature for 7 days from the first time you open it. After that, a one-time license key from Gumroad keeps it working on up to 3 Macs, with no subscription.

What happens if I don’t buy?

Cutwright’s Finder menu, Cut and Paste and ⌘L stop and show a short note; Finder itself works exactly as before, and Undo History can still take back anything Cutwright changed.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose License… from Cutwright’s menu bar menu (or License in the window’s sidebar), paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Cutwright checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from its menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
