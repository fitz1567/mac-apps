Writing Desk support

Write better anywhere on the web: readability, spell check, word count, snippets, AI prompts, case, posts, signatures.

The 9 tools

  • Readability: Grade level, reading time and highlights for hard sentences and passive voice, in the editor you write in or on the page. Free: grade level, counts and reading time; hard and very hard sentences and passive voice highlighted where you write; the scratch pad. Pro: adverbs, 259 wordy phrases and complex words with plainer versions, highlights inside plain text boxes, your target grade, a copyable report.
  • Spell check: Spell check every visible word of the page, not just text boxes: typos, doubled words and mix-ups like “could of”. Free: any page in US English, typos underlined and listed with suggestions, doubled words and mix-ups, 25 words of your own. Pro: British, Canadian and Australian English, an unlimited dictionary with import and export, a dictionary per site, a selected area only, a copyable report.
  • Word count: Words, characters, sentences, reading and speaking time for your selection, the text box you’re in or the article. Free: every count, reading and speaking time, the longest sentence, kept up to date while you type. Pro: a live counter on the text box, word and character goals, readability scores, keyword density, copy the stats.
  • Snippets: A private text expander: type ;sig and get your signature, with dates, the clipboard and fill-ins, on the sites you pick. Free: 10 snippets on 3 sites, {date}, {time}, {clipboard} and {cursor}, insert any snippet from the list on any page. Pro: unlimited snippets and sites, folders, fill-in forms, date math, case matching, TextExpander and Text Blaze import, time saved.
  • AI prompts: Your saved AI prompts with fill-in blanks, inserted into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any text box. Free: 15 prompts with blanks, choice lists and defaults, insert or copy, search and favorites. Pro: unlimited prompts, folders and tags, JSON and CSV import and export, a 40-prompt starter pack, a shortcut.
  • Change case: UPPER, lower, Sentence or Title Case on the text you selected in a box or editor, in place, with undo. Free: UPPER, lower, Sentence and Title Case in place, the right-click menu, the cycle shortcut, a convert-and-copy box. Pro: AP, Chicago, APA and MLA title case, your word lists, camelCase, snake_case and 8 more, smart quotes, tidy spaces, a shortcut per case.
  • Glossary: Your own glossary of approved terms: search, insert into the text box, make a term a snippet, check a page for wrong ones. Free: one glossary, one language pair, 100 terms; search, insert, copy, and any term as a snippet. Pro: unlimited terms, glossaries and languages, client tags, CSV and TBX import and export, the consistency check, term of the day.
  • Post drafts: Draft social posts with exact counts for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and more, previews, readability and spelling. Free: 10 drafts, exact counts for 7 networks, 2 previews at a time, readability, spelling and reading time for each draft. Pro: unlimited drafts with folders and search, every preview at once, the thread splitter, bold and italic text, hashtag sets, export.
  • Email signature: Design an email signature and copy it for Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail; keep its text as a ;sig snippet. Free: one signature, 2 templates, 6 colors, copy as rich text, HTML or plain text, paste guides, dark-mode checks. Pro: 10 templates, several signatures, a button and banner, any color and 8 fonts, a team batch from a spreadsheet, import and export.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Writing Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.
