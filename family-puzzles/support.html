Need a hand?

Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn’t, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

Profiles

How do I add someone?

Open the Family tab (in the sidebar on iPad and Mac) and tap Add Profile. Choose a kid with a grade, or a grown-up, then a picture, a color and a text size. The free version has 2 profiles; Family Pro has 8.

How do I switch profiles?

Tap the people button at the top of Home, then pick who’s playing.

Can the text be bigger?

Yes. Each profile has a text size (Standard, Large or Extra Large), and the app also follows your device’s text size setting.

Do profiles sync between my devices?

Yes, when your devices are signed in to the same iCloud account. Give it a minute after the first launch on a new device.

Puzzles and learning

What’s free?

Every game has a free puzzle every day and its first puzzles free, and every grade has free activities. Addition and subtraction in Math Facts are free.

Which grades are covered?

Kindergarten to Grade 8, with 20–24 activities in each grade. The lesson content is in English; the rest of the app is also in Spanish.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes, on a Mac or an iPad with a keyboard. Each game lists its keys on its own screen.

Can I change the read-aloud voice?

Yes. In Family, tap Choose a Voice… (a grown-up answers the sum first), pick English or Español, and tap Preview to hear each one. Automatic uses the nicest voice on your device. For nicer voices, free: on iPhone and iPad, Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices; on a Mac, System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System voice › Manage Voices…. Download one marked Enhanced or Premium and Automatic switches to it.

Family Pro

What does Family Pro add?

Every puzzle in every game, every activity in every grade, multiplication and division in Math Facts, the Challenge setting in lessons, and up to 8 profiles. It’s one purchase with no subscription, it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Family Sharing is on.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
