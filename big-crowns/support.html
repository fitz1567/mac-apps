Playing

What are the rules?

Place one crown in every row, every column and every region (the areas with thick borders). Crowns can’t touch each other, not even corner to corner. Tap a square once to mark it out (×), twice for a crown, and a third time to clear it.

Does every puzzle have one answer?

Yes. Each puzzle is checked when it’s made: it has exactly one solution, and it can be solved by logic alone, with no guessing.

How do hints work?

Tap Hint and Big Crowns shows the next step and names it, for example “Row 4 has only one open square” or “Region C is confined to column 2, so the rest of that column is out”. Tap Show Me to do the step. If a crown or mark doesn’t match the solution, the hint points it out first.

I find the colors hard to tell apart.

Every region has thick borders, its own pattern and a letter, so you never need the colors. In Settings, try the High Contrast theme, which uses white squares and stronger patterns.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes, on a Mac or an iPad with a keyboard: the arrow keys move, Space cycles a square, C places a crown, X marks a square out, Delete clears it and H shows a hint (press it again to do the step).

Printing and books

How do I print?

With Pro, tap the printer button on the home screen or Print This Puzzle in a puzzle’s menu. Print the puzzle on screen, or a pack of 6, 12 or 24 new puzzles at one size, with answer pages at the back. You can also save the pages as a PDF.

Are there printed puzzle books?

FitzTopPicks also makes large-print puzzle books (Sudoku, crosswords, word search, logic grids and more), sold on Amazon. Search for “Kevin Fitzgerald large print”.

Big Crowns Pro

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Today’s Puzzle and the first five puzzles at each size are free. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks endless puzzles at every size, printing and PDF packs, and the Garden, Ocean and Sunset themes. There’s no subscription, no ads and no coins, and Family Sharing is on. To restore it on a new device, tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
