Freelance Desk privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Freelance Desk is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your proposal templates and the bids you log (job title, link, platform, date, rate, credits and fees, status, result value, notes, follow-up date)
  • Your time-tracking clients, hourly rates and rules, and time entries for pages that match your rules: client, start and end time, the page address without its query string, and the page title
  • The meeting agenda you're timing and, with Pro, saved agenda templates, item owners and notes
  • Your business details and logo, invoice clients (name, address, email, tax ID), invoices and quotes with their lines, dates and payment status
  • Each tool's settings, and your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Freelance Desk never sends your bids, time, meetings, invoices or clients anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics. Pages that match none of your time-tracking rules are ignored and never stored; it never reads your meeting pages or fills in anything on freelance sites. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Freelance Desk sends the key and Freelance Desk's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: When the user clicks the toolbar button (or the right-click item) on a job post, Bids & proposals can read that one tab's title and address to start a bid entry (Clip this tab). Nothing on the page is read or changed, and inbox, payment and account pages are refused.
  • storage: Keeps each tool's data on this computer: proposal templates and logged bids; clients, rules and time entries; the meeting agenda being timed and saved templates; business details, logo, clients, invoices and quotes; settings and the Pro license.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Freelance Desk open in Chrome's side panel, next to a job post while writing a proposal or next to a video call while timing its agenda.
  • alarms: Wakes the extension once a minute to save the running time (Time tracking), at 9:00 on a bid's follow-up date (Pro), and every few hours to make a recurring invoice draft that is due (Pro). Nothing is sent automatically.
  • idle: Time tracking stops counting when the user has been away from the keyboard for a few minutes or the screen is locked, so idle time isn't billed.
  • contextMenus: Adds "Log this job in Freelance Desk" to the right-click menu, so the user can start a bid entry from a job post (with any text they selected) or a link to one.
  • tabs (optional): Never granted at install. Asked the first time the user opens Time tracking: it reads the address and title of the active tab while the browser is in the background, to check it against the user's own client rules (for example docs.google.com/document/d/abc → Client A). Pages that match no rule are ignored and never stored. Removable on the settings page.
  • notifications (optional): Asked only when the user turns on reminders: a follow-up reminder for a bid on the date they set, or a note that a recurring invoice draft is ready. No other notifications.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Freelance Desk isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
