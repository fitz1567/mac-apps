Freelance Desk support

For freelancers: proposal templates and a bid log, client time tracking, a meeting timer, and invoices saved as PDF.

The 4 tools

  • Bids & proposals: Proposal templates you fill in and copy, and a private log of every job you bid on. Free: 3 proposal templates with fields and counts, 25 logged bids with status, clip a job post from the tab or the right-click menu. Pro: unlimited templates and bids, win rate and cost per hire by platform, template, A/B tag and rate, follow-up reminders, CSV and JSON export.
  • Time tracking: Counts the time you spend on the sites and docs you assign to each client, and pauses when you’re away. Free: 3 clients and 10 rules, automatic counting with idle pause, a manual timer, today and this week, a timesheet to fix entries. Pro: unlimited clients, hourly rates and rounding, reports for any dates, CSV, a printable time summary.
  • Meeting timer: Paste a meeting agenda and time it: a countdown per item, a soft chime when one runs over and the projected end time. Free: one agenda at a time, full timing with chime, keyboard controls and the live end time. Pro: agenda templates, item owners and notes, a meeting summary (Markdown or text), the pop-out timer.
  • Invoices: Invoices and quotes saved as PDF, with paid and overdue tracking, taxes and any currency. Free: invoices with the Classic template, PDF, 3 saved clients, taxes and discounts in any currency, paid tracking for the 10 latest, backup. Pro: unlimited clients and every invoice, quotes that become invoices, recurring drafts, 3 more templates and any accent color, an income dashboard, CSV.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Freelance Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.
