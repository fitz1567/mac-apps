Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Textfolio does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Textfolio (“the app”), a Mac app: export Messages conversations to clean, dated PDFs, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Textfolio does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • A temporary copy of the Messages database, only while Textfolio is reading it
  • Your settings: your name for exports, paper size, cover page and photos on or off, and whether you’ve seen the welcome screen
  • Your license key (and the trial’s start date), in your Mac’s Keychain

Each time Textfolio reads your messages it copies the database into a private temporary folder, reads the copy and deletes it right away; a copy left by a crash is deleted the next time Textfolio opens. Settings are in Textfolio’s preferences, and the license key in your Keychain. Contact names are read into memory while the app runs and are saved only in the exports you make. Exports are new files, written only where you choose.

Your messages

Textfolio reads the Messages history on your Mac after you turn on Full Disk Access for it in System Settings. It copies the database and reads the copy read-only, so Messages is never changed. Textfolio runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox because a sandboxed app can’t read the Messages folder even with Full Disk Access. Nothing about your messages or contacts is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Contacts (optional)

If you choose Use Contacts, macOS asks whether Textfolio may access your contacts. It reads names, phone numbers and email addresses only to show names instead of numbers in the app and in your exports, keeps them in memory while it runs, never sends them, and saves names only in the exports you make. Without it, exports show the numbers. To turn it off, switch Textfolio off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts.

Network

Textfolio is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID) and checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com. Neither sends anything about your messages.

Updates and license checks

Textfolio, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Textfolio contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Textfolio is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
