Research Desk support

Collect and cite what you find on the web: Markdown clips, quotes with links, APA/MLA citations, highlights, tables, site notes.

The 6 tools

  • Markdown clip: Copy the article or your selection as clean Markdown, with tables, code blocks and links intact. Free: article or selection to Markdown, copied as it opens, title and link header, edit before copying, add it to Site notes. Pro: front matter for Obsidian, Logseq, Hugo and Notion, .md files, image and link options, right-click copy, your last 50 clips.
  • Table copy: Every table on the page, previewed; copy one to Excel, Sheets or Numbers, merged cells and headers handled. Free: list and preview every table, highlight it on the page, copy as TSV or CSV with no row limit, header rows. Pro: Markdown and JSON, .csv files, number cleanup, choose and rename columns, collect rows across pages, selection to table.
  • Quote & link: Copy the selected text as a quote with the page’s title and a link that jumps to that passage, with a citation if you like. Free: Markdown and plain text, title and link, passage links, an APA, MLA or Chicago citation under the quote, add it to Site notes. Pro: rich text and HTML, your own templates, author and site detection, the last 50 quotes.
  • Citations: An APA 7, MLA 9 or Chicago citation for the page you’re on, from its own metadata; fix any field, copy, save a bibliography. Free: all four styles, edit every field, copy with italics, save 10 citations and copy them as a bibliography. Pro: unlimited citations in projects, Word, RTF, RIS and BibTeX export, in-text citations and notes, DOIs found in the page.
  • Highlights: Highlight text on any page in colors, add notes, find them all again, and quote or cite any highlight. Free: highlights on 20 pages, 2 colors, notes, search, quote or cite a highlight, the list in the side panel. Pro: unlimited pages, 6 colors, tags, auto-show per site, Markdown and Obsidian export, JSON backup.
  • Site notes: A private note for each website, where your clips, quotes and citations from that site collect, with checklists and links. Free: 20 notes, one per site, Markdown with checklists, clips added from the other tools, the site’s highlights a click away. Pro: unlimited notes, notes for one page or a section, search, tags, colors and pins, a badge on sites with a note, export.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Research Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 6 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Research Desk reach the license server.
