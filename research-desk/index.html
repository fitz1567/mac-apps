Research Desk privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Research Desk is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your settings: Markdown header and front matter templates, quote formats and templates, citation style, table and number options
  • With Pro: your last 50 Markdown clips and last 50 quotes (page title, address and the text)
  • Citations you save (title, authors, date, publication, page address) and your project names
  • The text you highlight with a little text around it, your notes, tags and colors, and the address and title of each page you highlight on; with Pro, the sites you turned auto-show on for
  • With Pro: table rows you add to a collection, only until the browser closes
  • Your site notes, with the site, section or page address and title each belongs to, and their tags, colors and pins
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Research Desk never sends your clips, quotes, citations, highlights, tables or notes anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics, no citation lookups. It reads a page only when you use one of its tools on it. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Research Desk sends the key and Research Desk's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Lets the tools read the tab where the user clicked the toolbar button (or pressed its shortcut), and only that tab, only then: the article or selection for Markdown clip and Quote & link, the page's citation details, its tables, and the address and title Site notes files a note under.
  • scripting: When the user uses a tool on the page, runs that tool's own function in that tab: reading the article, selection, metadata or tables, drawing highlights with the CSS Custom Highlight API and the small note card, or outlining the table the user points at. It changes nothing on the page. With Pro, it also registers Highlights' script for the sites the user chose for auto-show.
  • storage: Keeps each tool's data on this computer: settings and templates, recent clips and quotes (Pro), saved citations and projects, highlights with their notes and the addresses and titles of those pages, table rows collected across pages (until the browser closes), and the site notes.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Research Desk open in Chrome's side panel next to the page they're reading, with their highlights and the site's notes.
  • contextMenus: Adds one right-click item, “Copy selection as Markdown”, when text is selected (Pro only), so a selection can be copied without opening the window.
  • tabs (optional): Never granted at install. Asked only when a Pro user turns on Site notes' badge reminder: it lets Research Desk see the address of the tab the user is on, so the toolbar button can show a dot when that site has a note. Turning the reminder off gives the permission back.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Research Desk isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
