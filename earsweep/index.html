EarSweep

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: EarSweep does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only thing EarSweep sends is your Pro license key, to check it with Gumroad.

This policy explains how EarSweep (“the app”), a web app for EQ ear training that runs in your browser, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. EarSweep does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind. The license check (below) passes your key to Gumroad and keeps nothing.

Information stored on your device, in your browser

To work, the app keeps the following on your device, in your browser only:

  • Your progress: your answers per band, your streak, daily drill results and history
  • Your settings: volume, sound, trainer, and whether you have read the listening tips
  • Songs you add to practice with (EarSweep Pro), so you can use them again
  • Your EarSweep Pro license key and when it was last checked, once you unlock Pro

It stays in your browser’s storage for EarSweep (IndexedDB) on your device and is never sent to us. In a private window it lasts only until you close the tab. To delete it, choose Erase my data on the Progress tab, or clear the site’s data in your browser’s settings.

Your songs

Songs you add are decoded and played by your browser on your device. They are never uploaded, sent to us or shared. EarSweep doesn’t include or stream anyone else’s music: every practice sound is made by the app’s own code as you play.

What it sends

Opening EarSweep loads its pages from our web server, like any website. After that, the only thing it sends is your EarSweep Pro license key: when you unlock Pro, and about once a week after that, it goes to EarSweep’s own server, which checks it with Gumroad’s license service and answers only whether Pro is on. The server keeps no copy of your key and never passes your email address or purchase details back to the app. EarSweep never uses your microphone or camera.

Hosting

EarSweep is hosted on Cloudflare, which delivers its pages and carries the license check to Gumroad. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the technical details of each connection (such as your IP address) to deliver it, under its own privacy policy. EarSweep itself keeps no record of who visits.

EarSweep Pro (purchases)

EarSweep is free to use. EarSweep Pro is a membership sold through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What you give Gumroad when you buy is covered by Gumroad’s privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact
