Using Tabbound

What files can I add?

PDFs and images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC and TIFF; each image becomes one page). Version 1.0 doesn’t read Word or Pages files: save them as PDF first. Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked first (open them in Preview and export a copy).

Do the index page numbers stay right?

Yes. Tabbound lays the index out, counts its pages, and works the page numbers out again until they hold, so the index’s own length is always counted. Every index line links to its document.

Is the text in my PDFs kept?

Yes. Pages are copied as they are, so text you could search or select before still can. Tabbound doesn’t run OCR on scans.

What happens when a document changes?

Save the bundle as a project. When a document changes, open the project and choose Bundle › Rebuild from Changed Files; the index, page numbers and bookmarks follow.

Can it do Bates numbering?

Yes: choose the Bates format in the Numbers pane and set the prefix, start number and digits, or start from the Bates numbering only preset.

Tabbound Pro

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Everything works without a key for bundles of up to 3 documents. Pro removes the limit. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Tabbound › Settings…, open the License tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Tabbound checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Tabbound menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
