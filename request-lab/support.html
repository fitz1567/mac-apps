Request Lab support

Modify request and response headers, mock API responses, and mark production, staging and local sites. Per-site access.

The 3 tools

  • Headers: Add, change or remove HTTP request and response headers on the sites you name, in profiles you switch in one click. Free: one profile with 5 request-header rules (set, append, remove), site and URL-filter matching, switch rules on and off, test an address. Pro: unlimited profiles and rules, quick profile switching, response headers (CORS, CSP), redirects, regex and request-type filters, tab-only profiles, export and import (also from ModHeader and Simple Modify Headers).
  • API mocks: Answer the API requests you choose with your own JSON or text, or make them fail, to test empty states and errors. Free: 3 mocks, a JSON editor that points at the error, JSON, text or network-error responses, method and page matching, a master switch. Pro: unlimited mocks, scenarios switched together (“Only this”), quick switches in the toolbar popup, export and import as JSON.
  • Environments: Mark production, staging and local copies of a site with a banner, a [PROD] tab title and a colored tab icon. Free: 3 marked sites with the five preset environments, a top banner, the title prefix and the toolbar label, the header profile and mocks for the site you’re on. Pro: unlimited sites in projects, your own labels, colors and environments, a tinted tab icon, banner position, size and opacity, confirm before submitting forms on production, export and import.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Request Lab. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Request Lab's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $19 once, not a subscription.
  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 3 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Request Lab reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.
