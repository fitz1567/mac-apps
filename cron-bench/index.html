Cron Bench privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Cron Bench is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your saved schedules (name, expression, dialect, time zone, tags and note)
  • The expression you're working on and your choices (dialect, time zone, snippet), so they're there next time
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Cron Bench never reads the pages you visit and never sends your schedules anywhere. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Cron Bench sends the key and Cron Bench's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: Shows the cron tester in Chrome's side panel, next to the code, docs or console the user is working in.
  • storage: Keeps the expression being edited, the user's preferences and their saved schedules on this computer.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Cron Bench isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
