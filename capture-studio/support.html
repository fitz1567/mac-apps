Capture Studio support

Full-page screenshots, text from images, bug reports, review pins and mockups of the tab you're on, with blur. Nothing uploaded.

The 8 tools

  • Screenshot: The visible area, a dragged area or the whole scrolling page, then arrows, boxes, text, blur and crop. Free: visible and area captures; arrows, boxes, highlights, text and crop; PNG download; the latest capture kept. Pro: full-page capture, blur (pixelate) that can’t be undone, JPEG and PDF, copy to the clipboard, your last 20 captures.
  • Record tab: Record the tab you’re on, with its sound and your microphone, to a WebM or MP4 file on your computer. Free: tab picture and sound, microphone, 720p or 1080p, WebM or MP4, pause, up to 5 minutes, no watermark. Pro: no time limit, a webcam bubble, click highlights, a 3-2-1 countdown, trim, GIF clips up to 15 seconds.
  • Grab text: Draw a box over an image, video, PDF or canvas and copy the text in it, read on your computer. Free: English, copy and edit the text, 50 grabs a day. Pro: unlimited grabs, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese, joined paragraphs, copy as a table, the last 50 grabs.
  • Bug report: Screenshot the tab, mark it up, redact private details and copy a ready bug report for any tracker. Free: capture, arrows, boxes, highlights, text, blur and redact, the full form with the environment, Markdown, copy image, PNG. Pro: Jira and plain-text formats, templates, numbered steps, crop, timed steps, the last 100 reports, editor shortcuts.
  • Review pins: Pin numbered comments on a web page and turn them into a screenshot report to print as PDF or send. Free: one page at a time, up to 10 pins, the full report with screenshots, PDF, Markdown, CSV and project files. Pro: projects with many pages, unlimited pins, statuses, your name on pins, a combined report, pins shown automatically per site.
  • Mockup: Put the tab in a browser, laptop, phone or tablet frame on a background, at the size a post or store needs. Free: the browser frame, 4 backgrounds, padding, corners and shadow, PNG at 1x or 2x, paste or open any image. Pro: laptop, phone, tablet and no frame, store and social sizes, 3 shots side by side, captions, marks and blur, every background, presets, JPEG and WebP.
  • Code image: Turn the code you select or paste into a clean image for posts, docs and slides, drawn on your computer. Free: 12 languages, 3 themes, 8 backgrounds, window frames, line numbers, PNG at 1x or 2x, copy and save. Pro: all 12 themes and your own colors, highlighted lines, redaction with secret finding, size presets, 4x PNG and SVG, presets.
  • Blur: Click names, emails and numbers on the page to blur them before a screenshot, recording or screen share. Free: click to blur and unblur, blur selected text, clear all, strength, blurs remembered on one site, put back before captures. Pro: blurs remembered on every site, auto-blur as pages load, email/phone/card/IBAN and your own patterns, form fields and images, presentation mode, show/hide shortcut.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Capture Studio. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Capture Studio's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $29 once, not a subscription.
  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 8 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Capture Studio reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.
