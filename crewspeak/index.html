Effective November 4, 2026

Privacy PolicySupportAll apps
The short version: Crewspeak does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

This policy explains how Crewspeak (“the app”), a jobsite Spanish and English phrasebook for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Crewspeak does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your iPhone or iPad

To work, the app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Your favorite phrases and their order
  • Your settings: the language you speak, slower speech and your chosen voices
  • Whether All Trades is unlocked

It stays in the app’s own storage on your iPhone (iOS’s standard app preferences). Your favorites list is also sent to your paired Apple Watch so it works with the phone away. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Speech

Phrases are spoken by your device’s own voices, on the device. Nothing is recorded, and the microphone is never used.

Works offline

Crewspeak makes no internet requests, so it works on any jobsite.

Purchases

Crewspeak is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: help@fitztoppicks.com.
