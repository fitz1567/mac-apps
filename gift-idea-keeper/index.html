Gift Idea Keeper privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Gift Idea Keeper is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • People you add (name, relationship, birthday, anniversary, sizes, likes and dislikes)
  • Gift ideas (title, link, price you typed, store, notes, status, occasion)
  • Budgets and settings
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Gift Idea Keeper never sends your lists anywhere and never reads the pages you visit or tracks prices. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Gift Idea Keeper sends the key and a device label (such as "Gift Idea Keeper on macOS") to Lemon Squeezy's license server to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: Shows the user's gift lists in Chrome's side panel, next to the page they're shopping on.
  • storage: Keeps the user's people, gift ideas, budgets and settings on this computer.
  • alarms: Schedules the reminders the user turns on (for example 14 and 3 days before a birthday or holiday).
  • activeTab: When the user clicks "Save this page as an idea" (or the right-click menu item), reads only that tab's title and web address to fill in a new idea. Nothing on the page is read, and cart, checkout, account, order and sign-in pages are refused.
  • contextMenus: Adds one right-click item, "Save this page as a gift idea", which opens the side panel with the idea filled in.
  • notifications: Optional, asked only when the user turns on desktop reminders: shows a notification before a birthday or holiday.
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Gift Idea Keeper isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
