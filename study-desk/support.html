Study Desk support

Study smarter in your browser: timestamped video notes, playlist study plans, flashcards, practice reviews, grades & GPA.

The 5 tools

  • Video notes: Timestamped notes beside the video you’re watching: click a time to jump back, turn a note into a flashcard. Free: notes on 10 videos, click a time to jump back, search, #tags, Markdown export, YouTube and HTML5 videos. Pro: any number of videos, frame screenshots, a notebook across videos with tag filters, export all as Markdown, HTML or PDF, backup, pause while typing.
  • Playlist pace: How long a YouTube playlist takes, at 1.25× to 2× and from the video you’re on, planned into daily study sessions. Free: total length, times at 1.25×–2× and your speed, what’s left from the current video, the card on YouTube (optional). Pro: study plans: daily sessions by minutes or days, a calendar with rest days and a target date, check-offs, notes counts, exports.
  • Flashcards: Select text on any page to make a flashcard, then review it with spaced repetition in its own tab. Free: 50 cards in one deck, front and back, keyboard review with undo, the count of cards due on the button. Pro: unlimited cards and decks, fill-in-the-blank and reverse cards, tags and search, stats, Anki and CSV export, CSV import, a daily reminder.
  • Practice log: Log the coding practice problems you solve with your notes and patterns, and review them before you forget. Free: 30 problems with notes, patterns, results and times, a spaced-repetition review queue, search and filters. Pro: unlimited problems, stats by pattern and difficulty with weak spots, a daily reminder, CSV/JSON export and import, a printable cheat sheet.
  • Grades & GPA: Your current grade, what you need on the final, and your GPA: weights, drop-lowest and extra credit done right. Free: one course with every grade feature, needed-on-the-final, the quick final calculator, one semester’s GPA. Pro: unlimited courses and semesters, what-if mode, a target GPA planner, a scale per course, export/import, a printable report.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Study Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Study Desk's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $15 once, not a subscription.
  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 5 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Study Desk reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.
