Study Desk privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Study Desk is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Video notes: each video's title and address, your note times and text, and frame screenshots if you add them (Pro); two settings (pause while typing, add frames)
  • Playlist pace: with Pro, the playlists you save (title, address, video titles, links and lengths), your plan settings, which videos you checked off and your notes; whether to show the length card on YouTube
  • Flashcards: your cards (front, back, deck, tags, and the title and address of the page or video note each was made from), their review schedule and history, and your settings
  • Practice log: the problems you log (title, link, site, difficulty, patterns, your notes, each attempt's result and time, review dates) and your settings
  • Grades & GPA: the courses, categories, weights and scores you type in, your semesters, credits and letter grades, a prior GPA if you enter one, and your grading scale and rounding settings
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Study Desk never sends your notes, cards, plans, problems or grades anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics. It never downloads videos and never calls YouTube itself; it reads only what the page you opened it on already shows. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Study Desk sends the key and Study Desk's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Gives Study Desk access to the current tab only when the user clicks its toolbar button or presses one of its shortcuts, so the page tools can work on that one page: Video notes reads the video's time and seeks it, Playlist pace reads the YouTube playlist's video lengths, Flashcards reads the selected text, and Practice log reads the problem's title and difficulty label.
  • scripting: After the user opens a tool on a page, runs that tool's small read-only helper there: Video notes' video helper (the main video's current time, length, play state, the page title and address; seeking, pausing while typing and grabbing a frame on request), Playlist pace's reader (the playlist's title and each listed video's title, link and length label), or a one-time function that returns the selected text (Flashcards) or the problem page's title, link, first heading and Easy/Medium/Hard label (Practice log). With YouTube access given and the length card turned on, Playlist pace's reader is registered for youtube.com so the card appears by itself. Nothing runs on a page before the user asks.
  • storage: Keeps each tool's data and settings on this computer: video notes (video title and address, note times and text, frame screenshots with Pro); saved playlist plans (playlist and video titles, links and lengths, plan settings, check-offs, notes); flashcards (both sides, deck, tags, source title and link) with their review schedule and history; the practice-problem log (titles, links, difficulty, patterns, notes, attempts, review dates); courses, scores, grading scale and GPA; reminder settings; and the Pro license.
  • sidePanel: Lets the user keep Study Desk open in Chrome's side panel beside a video or an article, for example to take timestamped notes while the video plays.
  • alarms: Updates the count of flashcards and practice problems due on the toolbar button after midnight and, for Pro users who turn it on, wakes Study Desk at the hour they chose for the daily review reminder. Nothing else runs on a timer.
  • unlimitedStorage: Pro users can save a small screenshot of the video frame with a note (about 30 KB each) and keep thousands of flashcards with their review history; these pass Chrome's default 10 MB local limit, and notes and cards must never be lost because of it. Nothing leaves the computer.
  • notifications (optional): Never granted at install. Asked for only when a Pro user turns on the daily review reminder in Flashcards or Practice log. Shows one notification a day, e.g. "12 cards to review". Without it, the number on the button still shows what's due.
  • https://www.youtube.com/* (optional): Never granted at install. Asked for only when the user chooses it: "Allow on YouTube" in Video notes (so the notes keep up with every YouTube video without a click on each one) or turning on Playlist pace's length card (so it appears on YouTube playlists by itself). Study Desk then reads only the playing video's time, the page title and address, and the playlist's video lengths shown on the page. Removable on the settings page; both tools still work without it on the tab where the user clicks the button.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/* (optional): Asked for only when the user activates a Pro license key, to check the key.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Study Desk isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
