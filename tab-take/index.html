Tab Take privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Tab Take is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Your recording settings
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Tab Take has no server and sends nothing anywhere. Recordings are made and kept in Chrome on your computer until you save them as a file. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Tab Take sends the key and Tab Take's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: When the user clicks the toolbar button (or presses the shortcut) on a tab, lets Tab Take record that one tab and show its title. No access to other tabs.
  • tabCapture: Records the picture and sound of the tab the user chose, after they press Start recording. Chrome shows its sharing icon on the tab while recording. The video is saved only on the user's computer; nothing is uploaded.
  • offscreen: Opens one hidden extension page (reason: USER_MEDIA) that holds the recording while it runs: it records the tab with MediaRecorder, plays the tab's sound back so the user still hears it, and mixes in the microphone or webcam if the user turned them on. It closes when the recording has been handed to the 'Recording ready' page.
  • storage: Keeps the user's recording settings (quality, format, microphone, webcam bubble position) and the license key on this computer.
  • scripting: Pro only, and only on the tab being recorded after the user's click: draws click highlights (a ring where the user clicks) and the 3-2-1 countdown on the page so they appear in the video. It doesn't read the page or change anything else, and is removed when recording stops.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Tab Take isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
