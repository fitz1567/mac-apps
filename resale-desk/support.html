Resale Desk support

For resellers: fees, payout and profit on 9 marketplaces, and a listing checker for title limits and banned words.

The 2 tools

  • Profit & fees: Type a sale price and your costs: every marketplace fee line by line, your payout, profit, margin, ROI and break-even price. Free: the full calculator on any one marketplace at a time (eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Facebook, Amazon, Whatnot, Shopify). Pro: all 9 side by side, target price for the profit or margin you want, saved items, bulk CSV, dimensional weight, editable fee tables.
  • Listing check: Paste a listing draft and see each marketplace’s title limits, banned and discouraged words, repeats, capitals, links and keyword coverage as you type. Free: 2 marketplace profiles of your choice, every live check, your own banned words and acronyms, copy each field, one listing. Pro: all 7 profiles and your own, editable rules, saved listings and templates, keyword lists per product type, bulk CSV check, title builder.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to Resale Desk. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • Buy: open Resale Desk's settings (right-click its icon → Options) and click Buy Pro. It's $15 once, not a subscription.
  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 2 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let Resale Desk reach the license server.
  • Another computer: a key works on up to 3 of your browsers. Entering it again on a browser where it was already activated doesn't use another one. Need a 4th? Email help@fitztoppicks.com with your Gumroad receipt and we'll free a place.
  • Lost your key: search your email for "Gumroad", or find the purchase in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library) if you have a Gumroad account.
  • Refunds: within 14 days of buying, email help@fitztoppicks.com with your order number.

Email help@fitztoppicks.com with what you tried, the page you were on (if you're comfortable sharing it) and your Chrome version (chrome://version).
