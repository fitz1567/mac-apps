The short version: Snapwright does not collect your data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and none of your work is sent to us. The license and update checks are described below.

This policy explains how Snapwright (“the app”), a Mac app: Windows-style window snapping, zones, a taskbar and live crops, developed by Kevin Fitzgerald, handles information.

Information we collect

None. Snapwright does not collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server, and it does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app keeps the following on your Mac:

  • Your zone sets, snap layouts, workspaces and the remembered window layout for each display setup
  • Your settings (shortcuts, taskbar, snapping options)
  • Your license key, in your Mac’s Keychain

Layouts and settings are in a folder in Application Support and in preferences on your Mac, and the license key is in your Keychain. Crop and preview pictures are kept in memory only and never saved.

Your Mac

Snapwright runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox because it moves other apps’ windows. With your permission (Accessibility) it reads window positions, sizes and titles to move them. It records no typing: while one of its pop-ups is open it looks only for the keys that close or use it, such as Esc and the number keys. Screen Recording is optional and is used only for pictures in taskbar previews, crops and the Mouse Jump map; those pictures stay in memory.

Network

Snapwright is sold directly through Gumroad, and its only network requests are the license check (when you enter a key, then each time the app opens and about once a week: Gumroad gets your key, and our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, gets a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself, to see whether it was reset; both get the app’s product ID), checks for app updates at fitztoppicks.com, and the list of our other Mac apps (More Mac Apps), downloaded from fitztoppicks.com when you open it in Help (at most once a day; the same file for everyone). None of them sends anything about your windows.

Updates and license checks

Snapwright, downloaded directly from Gumroad or fitztoppicks.com and signed with Apple’s Developer ID, checks for app updates with Sparkle, a standard open-source updater: about once a day it downloads a small update list (an appcast) from fitztoppicks.com, and it downloads an update from fitztoppicks.com only when one is available and you choose to install it (or have turned on automatic updates).

Until you enter a license key, Snapwright contacts neither Gumroad nor our license page. When you enter one, it sends that key and the app’s product ID to Gumroad to verify it, and checks it again each time it opens and about once a week while it stays open. Gumroad’s answer (including the email address you bought with) is kept with the key in your Mac’s Keychain. With each of those later checks it also asks our license page, licence.fitztoppicks.com, whether the key was reset there (the page is where you free a Mac). That question contains only the app’s product ID and a fingerprint of the key (a one-way SHA-256 code made from it), never the key itself, your name or your email address, and the page doesn’t save it.

None of these requests contains anything about your files or how you use the app. As with any web request, the server also sees your internet address and ordinary details such as the app’s name and version, your macOS version and your language.

Purchases

Snapwright is sold directly through Gumroad, which handles payment and sends your receipt and license key; we never see your payment details. What the license check sends is under Updates and license checks above.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
