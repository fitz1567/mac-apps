Using Inkdelta

Which files can it compare?

Word documents (.docx) and PDFs, in any mix: two PDFs, two Word files, or a PDF against a Word file. Older .doc files need to be saved as .docx first. Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked in Preview first. In Spreadsheet mode it compares Excel (.xlsx), CSV and TSV and Numbers files cell by cell, values and formulas separately; Numbers files are exported to Excel by Numbers itself, so Numbers needs to be installed. Old .xls files need to be saved as .xlsx first.

What does it compare?

The text: paragraphs, table cells, headers and footers. Page layout, images and formatting (bold, fonts, colors) aren’t compared. Changed numbers, dates, amounts and percentages are picked out and listed on their own.

What does “OCR” mean on a page?

That page had no text in it (it was scanned), so Inkdelta read it with on-device text recognition. OCR can misread a character, so check changes on those pages against the original.

Why does a PDF show changes I didn’t make?

PDFs don’t always keep text in reading order, especially with columns or text boxes. Try Ignore Spacing and Ignore Headers and Footers, and compare the Word files when you have them.

Can I get Word’s tracked changes?

Not yet. Export the redline as a Word file (colored, struck-through and underlined text), HTML or RTF, or the change report as a PDF.

Is this legal advice?

No. Inkdelta shows what changed in the text; it doesn’t judge what a change means.

Inkdelta Pro

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Everything works without a key for documents up to 5 pages each and spreadsheets up to 250 rows per sheet. Pro removes both limits. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Inkdelta › Settings…, open the Pro tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Inkdelta checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Inkdelta menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
