Problem Review privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Problem Review is a Chrome extension by Kevin Fitzgerald. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours: what you save stays in your browser on this computer.

What it keeps on your computer

  • The problems you log: title, link, site, difficulty, patterns, your notes, each attempt's result and time, review dates
  • Your settings (daily reminder on/off and hour)
  • Your license key, if you buy Pro

This lives in the browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network

Problem Review never sends your log anywhere and reads a page only when you click its button. The only network request is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad, when you activate it and every two weeks.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold by Gumroad, which handles the payment and emails your license key. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers that purchase. When you activate a key, Problem Review sends the key and Problem Review's Gumroad product ID to Gumroad's license server (api.gumroad.com) to check it, and checks it again about every two weeks. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: Reads the problem page only in the tab where the user clicked the toolbar button, and only then.
  • scripting: When the user clicks the toolbar button, runs one function in that tab that reads the page title, link, first heading and a visible Easy/Medium/Hard label. It never reads the code editor, the problem text or account details, and changes nothing on the page.
  • storage: Keeps the user's problem log (titles, links, difficulty, patterns, notes, attempt results, review dates) and settings on this computer.
  • alarms: Updates the toolbar count of problems due for review after midnight and, for Pro users who turn it on, wakes the extension at the hour they chose for the daily review reminder.
  • notifications: Optional, asked for only when a Pro user turns on the daily review reminder. Shows one notification a day, e.g. "4 problems due for review". No other notifications.
  • api.gumroad.com (asked only when you activate a key): to check your Pro license key.

Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold or shared. No data is used for advertising, credit or lending.

Children

Problem Review isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
