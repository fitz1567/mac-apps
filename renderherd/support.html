Using Renderherd

How do I add a helper Mac?

Install Renderherd on it and open Share This Mac; it shows a 6-digit code for 2 minutes. On the controller, pair with that Mac and type the code. Both Macs show check digits; click Matches on both.

Why did a Mac sit a job out?

It doesn’t have the Blender version the job is pinned to, it’s set to render only when idle or on power, or it failed three chunks in a row. Its lane says which.

Do helper Macs need a key?

No. Only the Mac that runs the queue needs one.

Renderherd license

What’s free, and what does a key add?

Without a key you can use this Mac plus 1 helper with 3 unfinished jobs. Pro adds every paired Mac, an unlimited queue, shared-folder mode, make-a-movie and hooks, on up to 3 Macs; Shop is the same on up to 5. It’s a one-time purchase on Gumroad, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose Renderherd › Settings…, open the License tab, paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How do I get updates?

Renderherd checks for updates on its own; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the Renderherd menu. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
