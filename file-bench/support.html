File Bench support

Merge and sign PDFs, compress images, trim audio, open big CSVs, make favicons and QR codes. Never uploaded.

The 8 tools

  • PDF pages: Merge PDFs, then reorder, rotate, delete, extract and split their pages, with no upload. Free: merge up to 3 PDFs (20 MB each), drag to reorder, rotate and delete pages, undo, extract the pages you pick. Pro: any number of files of any size, split by ranges or one file per page, page numbers, text watermark, compress, blank pages, extract images.
  • Fill & sign: Fill in and sign a PDF: text, dates, checkmarks and your signature, saved as a new copy on your computer. Free: one PDF at a time: text, dates, ✓ and ✗, one drawn or typed signature, save the signed PDF. Pro: saved signatures and initials, unlimited signature spots, initials on every page, fill and flatten form fields, templates, sign a stack of PDFs at once, signatures from a photo.
  • CSV viewer: Open big CSV and TSV files in a fast grid: sort, filter, search, copy to a spreadsheet and export. Free: files up to 50,000 rows, sort, filters, search, copy cells, selection sums, CSV export of the view. Pro: any number of rows, multi-column sort, any-rule filters, find and remove duplicates, column stats, hide and rename columns, join two files, split into a zip, saved views, JSON export.
  • Compress images: Compress, resize and convert photos to JPEG, WebP or AVIF, and remove their GPS location. Free: one image at a time, JPEG and WebP, quality and size, before/after compare with zoom, metadata report, all metadata removed. Pro: batch to a ZIP, target file size, AVIF and small PNGs, presets, file name patterns, remove location only.
  • Favicons: A website’s favicon.ico, Apple, Android and PWA icons and HTML tags from an image, letters or an emoji. Free: image, letters or emoji, background, shape and padding, the standard set (favicon.ico, Apple, Android, maskable, manifest, tags) in one zip, all previews. Pro: favicon.svg with dark mode, maskable tuning, extra sizes and Windows tiles, browser-extension icons, saved projects, batch.
  • Share images: Design Open Graph and social share images from templates, at the exact size each site wants. Free: 3 templates, Open Graph 1200×630 PNG, your title, logo and colors, 6 fonts, text that fits itself, contrast and safe-area checks. Pro: all 12 templates, X, LinkedIn, square and Pinterest sizes, every size in one zip, batch from CSV, brand kits, saved designs, background photos, JPEG and WebP, fill from the page you’re on.
  • QR codes: Static QR codes that never expire, for this page, links, text and Wi-Fi, as PNG or SVG. Free: a code for the page you’re on, links, text and Wi-Fi, colors with a contrast check, error correction, PNG and SVG. Pro: contact cards, email, SMS, phone, events and places, rounded and dot styles, a logo in the middle, batch from CSV, print sheets, saved designs.
  • Audio trim: Trim, fade, join and convert audio files, sample-accurate, saved as WAV, Opus or AAC. Free: one file up to 10 minutes, waveform, trim, delete, fades, gain, undo, loudness readout, WAV export. Pro: no length limit, join up to 50 files with crossfades, Opus and AAC export, normalize loudness, remove silence, batch, ringtones.

Install

Add it from your browser's store (the Chrome Web Store, or Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons where it's listed), then pin it. In Chrome or Edge: click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) icon in the toolbar and the pin next to File Bench. In Firefox: click the Extensions button and choose “Pin to Toolbar”.

Pro

  • One key, every tool: Pro unlocks all 8 tools at once; every tool also has a free version.
  • Activate: paste the license key from your purchase email into the settings and click Activate. Chrome asks once to let File Bench reach the license server.
