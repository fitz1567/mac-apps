Using Updatewright

Why does an app say “No feed”?

It has no update source Updatewright can read (no Sparkle feed, Homebrew cask, GitHub release or App Store receipt). Its own updater still works as before; check the developer’s website.

Does Updatewright install updates by itself?

No. Each update is handed to something you can see: the app’s own updater, a Terminal window that runs the brew command only after you press Return, or the App Store app. Update All That Are Safe does that one app at a time.

How does Moving day work?

On the old Mac, Copy Settings copies the settings you tick to a folder on a drive with a manifest. On the new Mac, New Mac Check opens that folder and lists the apps to install and the settings not there yet. The Restore Checklist puts it all in order.

Updatewright license

What does the trial include?

Every feature for 7 days from the first time you open it. After that, checking for updates, Update All That Are Safe, Copy Settings and New Mac Check need a license key; your last list, History, holds, Inventory and Restore Checklist stay open. A key is a one-time purchase on Gumroad for up to 3 Macs, with no subscription.

Where do I enter my license key?

Choose License in Updatewright’s sidebar (or License… in its menu bar menu), paste the key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate.

How does Updatewright update itself?

Choose Updatewright › Check for Updates…; it also checks on its own about once a day. Its own row in the list uses the same updater, and it’s never part of Update All That Are Safe. Updates are free.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Gumroad handles payment. Reply to your Gumroad receipt or email help@fitztoppicks.com.

Do I have to pay again on another Mac?

No. One license key works on up to 3 of your Macs. Download the app again from your Gumroad receipt or library and enter the same key.
