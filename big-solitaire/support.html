Playing

How do I move a card?

Tap it. It goes to the best place it can: up to its foundation (the four piles at the top right, ace to king) if it can, otherwise onto a column. Tap the stock (top left) to turn over cards; when it runs out, tap it again to turn the waste back over.

What are the rules?

Build the four foundations up from ace to king, one per suit. In the columns, stack cards down in alternating colors (a red 6 on a black 7); only a king can go in an empty column. Win by getting every card onto the foundations.

Can every deal be won?

In Draw 1, yes: every deal the app gives you was won by its built-in solver first. Draw 3 (in Settings) is harder, and not every Draw 3 deal can be won.

I’m stuck. What can I do?

Tap the light bulb for a hint (it highlights a card to move, or the stock), and use Undo as often as you like. When every card is face up, a Finish button puts them all away for you.

Can the cards be easier to read?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Extra large card labels, and Four-color suits to make diamonds blue and clubs green. On iPhone, turn it sideways for bigger cards; on a Mac, make the window bigger.

Deals

How many deals are free?

The first 20 deals and Today’s Deal, every day. A one-time purchase unlocks all 1,000 deals.

Does everyone get the same Today’s Deal?

Yes. Everyone gets the same deal on the same day.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to pay again on another iPhone or iPad?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.
